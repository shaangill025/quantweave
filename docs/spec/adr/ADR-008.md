# ADR-008: Research and performance claims

Status: adopted design baseline, implementation not verified. Date: 2026-10-08. Decision lineage: Q24,Q29,Q40,Q59,Q64,Q65.

## Context
One validation badge encourages false inference; synthetic examples do not prove edge.

## Decision
Separate operational correctness, investment evidence and user eligibility.

## Consequences and acceptance
Family-specific preregistered gates, complete trials, PIT and prospective evaluation.

## Revisit trigger
New measured evidence, provider/legal conditions or explicit requirement changes justify a superseding ADR. Record impact and preserve earlier lineage; never silently weaken required first-release scope.
