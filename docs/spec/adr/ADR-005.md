# ADR-005: Proposal authority and atomic final check

Status: adopted design baseline, implementation not verified. Date: 2026-10-08. Decision lineage: Q15,Q23,Q26,Q49,Q65,Q69,Q74.

## Context
A good thesis is not authority to consume stale or shared cash.

## Decision
Two origins, separate AI evaluation, deterministic final transaction over account/policy/strategy revisions.

## Consequences and acceptance
Material revisions invalidate dependent checks; zero actions is valid.

## Revisit trigger
New measured evidence, provider/legal conditions or explicit requirement changes justify a superseding ADR. Record impact and preserve earlier lineage; never silently weaken required first-release scope.
