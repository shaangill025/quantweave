### Rules (the module docstring has the detail)
- **Preselected, version-pinned baselines.** An `EvaluationPlan` holds `BenchmarkPolicy` baselines (each pins its series version) and `frozen_at`. `frozen_at` must be before the start date begins anywhere (00:00 at UTC+14, the same rule as `simulate_benchmark`), else `baseline_selected_late`. A baseline selected after `frozen_at` is `baseline_selected_after_freeze`. `revise` appends version + 1 and is refused once the period has begun (`baseline_change_after_start`) or with a time earlier than the current plan (`revision_out_of_order`). The scorecard records the plan id/version and each baseline as `policy@version:series@version`.
- **Actual side from the journal only.** External flows are the cash leg of `external_capital` and `transfer_clearing` postings of the account's events effective after the opening valuation and at or before the ending one. A transfer is external because the scope is one account (spec §5). Reversals net out because the fold reads postings. Flows are bucketed by date in the plan's reporting calendar (`tz`); same-date flows are netted; a flow on the end date is outside [start, end) (`flow_on_end_date`), as in `simulate_benchmark`. Since round 2, the caller passes the history and market inputs only: both valuations are `value_account(materialize(history, at))`, and the flows come from the same history, so they cannot come from different journal states. The instants must fall on the start and end dates (`valuation_date_mismatch`). A partial valuation keeps its `Unavailable` code.
- **No reconstruction from snapshots.** A `SourceSnapshot` anywhere in the actual history gives `snapshot_not_history`; holdings snapshots, mirror snapshots included, are never turned into trades or flows. A `VirtualEntry` in the actual history is a `TypeError`.
- **Deposits are flows.** Every side reports investment gain = ending - opening - net flows, contributions and withdrawals separately, Modified Dietz (labelled approximate, since there are no per-flow valuations) and MWR over the same dated flows. A baseline also reports its exact TWR from `simulate_benchmark`. The actual side shows income and standalone fee postings; trade fees are in trade cost and proceeds (spec §5) and so in the gain.
- **Cash-flow matching.** The virtual side (`value_virtual`: virtual cash plus units, each through `value_position` with a fresh mark and explicit multiplier) is valued from the same `MarketInputs` as the actual side (so the instants are shared by construction), must open at the actual opening value (`opening_not_matched`) and carry the same net capital per date (`cash_flows_not_matched`). Each baseline invests the actual opening value and flows. If the actual side is unavailable, its flows and opening are not established, so the virtual side and every baseline are `actual_unavailable` (renamed in round 1; the reason names the actual code): no flow is fabricated.
- **No real/virtual mixing.** Comparisons are gain differences against a baseline only (actual - baseline, simulated - baseline). No figure combines actual and simulated records.
- **Labels.** `scope` is the one account and `full_financial_situation` is always False (R042). Active-return attribution is not defined by the spec, so `attribution` says `not_defined_by_spec:active_return_allocation` and nothing is computed. `interpretation` is "descriptive; no significance or edge claim"; no `eval_stats` interval is attached in this increment.
- Single currency per plan: plan, baselines, valuations and virtual book share it (`CurrencyMismatchError`); a foreign-currency flow is `flow_currency_unsupported`, foreign income or fees are `Unavailable` for that figure.

## Review fixes (round 1)
The cross-model review returned REQUEST CHANGES (probes in the orchestrator's scratchpad). Each new test was run against the pre-fix module (saved copy) and failed there: 4 new tests failed, along with 2 existing tests whose expected downstream code changed.
- **S1 (baseline swap through `dataclasses.replace`).** `EvaluationPlan.digest` (init=False) is a sha256 over id, version, currency, period, zone key, `frozen_at` and the repr of every baseline field (selected_at and versions included). It is recomputed on every construction, `replace` included. `Scorecard` records `plan_digest` and `plan_frozen_at`. Test: a swapped plan with the same id and version has another digest, and the scorecard carries it. `replace` is not blocked (`revise` uses it); the digest makes the swap visible. `frozen_at` and the revision time are caller-asserted: there is no trusted clock. A persisted append-only plan registry is remaining work.
- **S2 (valuations not bound to history).** `_explained` re-values each real valuation from `materialize(history, as_of)` with the valuation's own marks, multipliers and FX through `value_account`. The result must equal the given `AccountValuation`, else `history_valuation_mismatch`. Foreign cash with no FX quote in the valuation gives `history_valuation_unverifiable` (this branch is untested). The reviewer's probe (history without the day-10 deposit, valuations with it, which gave gain 600 against a truth of 100) is now a test and returns `history_valuation_mismatch`.
- **N1.** The downstream code is now `actual_unavailable`, and its reason names the actual code (e.g. `mark_stale`). Test with a 2 h old mark against a 1 h max age. Baselines are still not computed when the actual side is unavailable, because the opening and flows are then not verified.
- **N2 to N6 (documented in the module docstring and here).** A `VirtualValuation` is trusted as given and should be built with `value_virtual`. An in-kind transfer counts at its recorded cost, not its market value. Duplicates are expected to be removed by `Journal.events()` (idempotency keys), and a raw sequence with a repeated event is the caller's error. Baseline levels are end-of-day while the real valuations are at their instants. A flow on the end date is refused rather than placed.
- **N7.** A virtual portfolio sharing the real account id is a `ValueError` (test).
