## Changes (4 code and test files, plus this receipt)
- **`qw_domain/option_packages.py` (new)**
  - **`PackageKind`:** the eight structures. Their values equal `options_catalogue.json` `allowed`, and a test checks this.
  - **`build_package` refusals** (each a typed `Unavailable`):
    - **Leg shape:** `leg_count`, `duplicate_contract`, `currency_mismatch`, `fee_negative`, `underlying_mismatch`, `right_mismatch`.
    - **Terms:** unverified or unknown terms, through T034 `contract_terms` (R089).
    - **Signs and order:** `quantity_sign`, and `strike_order` when a vertical's long leg is on the wrong side or the strikes are equal.
    - **Premiums:** `premium_sign` when a long receives or a short pays (journal sign), and `premium_zero` when a long leg has a premium of 0, even when fees are above 0.
    - **Verticals:** `expiry_mismatch`, `terms_mismatch` (units, cash, multiplier, settlement), `quantity_mismatch`, `style_cannot_cover`.
    - **Covered call:** `stock_leg_required`, `stock_quantity_mismatch` (exactly N units per short), `stock_basis_invalid` (basis ≤ 0), `cash_settled_call`. Other kinds refuse a stock leg with `stock_leg_unexpected`.
    - **Bounds:** `non_arbitrage` when max loss ≤ 0, for example a debit vertical bought at a net credit. `no_profit_possible` when max profit ≤ 0, for example a debit at or above the width. A covered call is exempt from `no_profit_possible`: on shares held above strike + premium the basis is sunk, so a non-positive `max_profit` is reported as is. A short leg may carry a zero premium.
  - **Coverage:** with an `AccountCover`, coverage runs T034 `check_cover`, and any blocking reason gives `coverage_refused`. With `cover=None` the package is analysis only, and live pricing refuses it. T026 and T020 wiring must pass an account.
  - **`payoff(S)`:** exact whole-package expiry P&L. It sums signed intrinsic from N·S + C − K·M, plus stock value minus basis, plus premiums in journal sign, minus fees.
  - **Bounds and breakevens:** taken from S = 0, every effective strike and the slope beyond the last kink.
    - Breakevens are exact `Fraction`s.
    - `max_profit` is None when unbounded.
    - `max_loss` is a positive amount.
    - Profit rounds down and loss rounds up.
- **`qw_domain/option_pricing.py` (new): `price_package`**
  - **Every use** needs a quote per leg for its own contract, in the strike currency and fresh, with bid and ask > 0 and bid ≤ ask. The feed must pass T021 `check_use`: `DERIVED_DATA` for research, `FINANCIAL_RECOMMENDATION` for live use.
  - **Live use** also needs:
    - a `REAL_TIME` latency class (indicative or delayed gives `feed_not_executable`, and an unknown class gives `feed_latency_unknown`);
    - an `AccountCover` (`coverage_not_checked`).
  - **Repricing:** each leg is priced at the conservative side, paying the ask and receiving the bid. The package is then **rebuilt with those premiums**, so the returned `package` bounds and breakevens belong to the priced premium (review finding 1). Any refusal of the rebuild blocks.
- Tests: `test_option_packages.py` (51 cases) and `test_option_pricing.py` (9 cases).

## Interpretations and spec gaps for the owner

- Covered calls and cash-secured puts are classed as bullish credit structures.

## Increment 2: pre-expiry scenario grid and zero-day expiry
Branch `t035-scenario-grid`, base `f3c0bf8`. Stdlib-only domain code. Nothing submits or exercises anything. The worker did not commit.

### Changes
- **`qw_domain/option_scenarios.py` (new): `scenario_grid`**
  - **Shocks:** the full product of underlying shocks (relative, > −1), implied-volatility shifts (absolute, applied to every leg) and elapsed time (≥ 0).
  - **Pricing:** before the expiry session's close, every leg is repriced with T034 `bsm` (decimal_math, precision 40), using each leg's own volatility. At or after the close (the expiry slice), every leg is worth its exact intrinsic value.
  - **Per-point outputs**, kept separate:
    - `premium_value`: option legs, + long.
    - `delta_units`: option deltas plus held shares. At expiry this is N units per in-the-money contract.
    - `pnl`: whole package, in the increment-1 payoff convention. On the expiry slice it equals `OptionPackage.payoff(S)` exactly.
    - `change`: against the unshocked base.
  - **Grid outputs:**
    - the T034 collateral (`cash_required`);
    - `worst_loss` = max(0, −pnl) over the base and every point. It is 0 when every point is profitable, and None when the grid has a gap, because a gap may hide the worst point;
    - `complete`;
    - `model_applicable` (False when an American leg uses the European approximation).
  - An empty shock list is refused with `no_shocks`.
  - **Failures:**
    - The base point must price, or the grid is `Unavailable`: `contract_expired`, `vol_missing`, a stale or future spot, `expiry_session_mismatch`, or unknown terms.
    - A refused point (for example, a non-positive shocked volatility) stays as a `ScenarioGap`. It is never zero-filled, and the grid is then incomplete.
    - A shocked spot keeps the base mark's age.
- **`qw_domain/option_pricing.py`:** live use now takes the trade's exchange session (`session: SessionRef`, from the calendar).
  - The session must be within one day of the UTC date of `at`; otherwise `trade_session_mismatch`.
  - A leg expiring on or before the later of the session date and the UTC date of `at` is blocked as `zero_day_expiry`, per `options_catalogue.json` `excluded_new_proposals`. Using the later date is conservative, so a stale session cannot hide a same-day expiry.
  - A missing session gives `trade_session_unknown`, and another calendar gives `trade_session_calendar_mismatch`.
  - Research use is unaffected.
- **Tests:** `test_option_scenarios.py` (14 cases) and two new cases in `test_option_pricing.py`.

### Oracles and properties
- NUM08 to NUM15 on the expiry slice: from a base spot of 50, a ±20% shock reaches the oracle's terminal spot, and the point's `pnl` equals the oracle `expiry_pnl`. NUM12 and NUM13 use the stated split of short +3.00 and long −1.00 against the net 2.00 credit.
- Hull's S = 42, K = 40, r = 10%, volatility 20%, T = 0.5 gives a base premium value of 476 (4.76 per unit).
- Put-call parity: a 40/45 bull call plus a 45/40 bear put is worth 475.614712250357 to 1e-9 at every spot and volatility shock.
- The long call is monotone in spot and in volatility.
- Covered call (hand-computed): at expiry, S = 40 gives P&L −600 with delta 100, and S = 60 gives 400 with delta 0. One minute before the close is within 0.01 of the expiry value. Collateral is 0 because the shares cover the call.
- **Hypothesis property** (120 derandomized examples): all eight kinds, random strikes and non-zero premiums, 1 to 4 spot shocks from −90% to +300%, volatility 10% to 80%, and time at 0, T/2 and expiry.
  - Every expiry-slice P&L equals `payoff(S)` exactly.
  - The grid's worst loss is at least max(0, base loss, every expiry-slice loss), and never exceeds the package's bounded `max_loss` (with r ≥ 0 and q = 0).
- **Interpretation:** "worst loss never better than the bounded max loss" is read as two checks. The worst loss is bounded by `max_loss`. The expiry slice reproduces the payoff, so its worst loss equals `max_loss` whenever the grid contains a price where the bound is attained.

### Negative cases
- A volatility shock to −0.2 against 0.2 gives the `vol_not_positive` gap. The expiry-slice point at that shock still prices, because intrinsic value needs no volatility.
- A base with no volatility, a stale spot, a base at the close, or a session for another date is refused.
- An American leg gives `model_applicable` False.
- A spot shock of −1 and negative elapsed time raise errors.
- Zero-day expiry, an unknown session and another calendar block live use only.
- With legs expiring today, yesterday's, today's and tomorrow's sessions all give `zero_day_expiry`. A session two days old gives `trade_session_mismatch`.
- A grid of only profitable points reports a worst loss of 0. A grid with a gap reports None. An empty shock list is refused.
