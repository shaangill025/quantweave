## Changes
- **`qw_domain/evidence.py` (new)**: `EvidenceStore`, append-only and tenant-scoped (every lookup keyed by (tenant, id); no mutation may be earlier than the tenant's last).
  - **Sources** enter only through T023 `require_ingest` (T021 retention and derived-data rights at receipt) and keep feed, feed revision hash, content SHA-256, publication and receipt times, retention end (receipt + granted `retain_for`), scope, jurisdiction and an **origin group** (SHA-256 of case/whitespace-normalized text, or a declared `same_origin_as`).
  - **Passages**: exact spans (the quote must equal the stored text at the offset) with a text hash and the structured `Reading` (entity, attribute, period, unit, Decimal value) they state. The reading value must appear in the quote as a number token, compared by Decimal value. Grouped numbers parse as one value when every group after the first has exactly three digits and one separator is used throughout: comma, space, NBSP, thin space (U+2009), narrow NBSP (U+202F) or apostrophe (`1 000` = `1'000` = 1000; `12 345.67` = 12345.67). A token touching another digit group across a separator (`1 00`, `2025 100`, `1,000 000`) produces nothing, so such text supports no value. `100` does not match inside `1000`, `1,0000`, `100A7` or `FY2025`. Otherwise `reading_not_in_quote`. `add_fact` builds its passage directly without this check (its reading is derived from the fact); the public `add_passage` has no bypass.
  - **Claims**: `reported_fact` only, append-only revisions with knowledge times; `claim_at` is the as-of query.
  - **Citations**: validated against the claim revision current at link time; refused on any entity, attribute, period (date), unit or value mismatch, a social source, a source not yet published (`not_yet_published`: max(publication, receipt) after the link time), or a withdrawn, deleted or expired source. Ids are SHA-256 of (tenant, claim, revision, passage): stable, never reused for other content.
  - **`support(tenant, claim, at, registry)`** counts a citation only if linked by `at`, bound to the revision current at `at`, and its source at `at` is published, undeleted, inside retention, has no withdrawn copy in its origin group and still passes `check_use(DERIVED_DATA)`. Otherwise `unsupported` with a per-citation reason (`revision_superseded`, `not_yet_published`, `withdrawn`, `deleted`, `retention_expired`, `rights_revoked`); claims are never removed. `independent_sources` counts origin groups, `corroborated` needs two, `replay_limited` flags deleted or purged cited content (also for a past `at`).
  - **Withdrawal** (publisher retraction) covers the origin group; **deletion** tombstones one copy and drops its content; `purge_expired` drops content past retention.
  - **Re-ingest**: `add_source` with an existing id and identical feed, kind, publication time and content returns the existing source (receipt time not reset); anything else under that id is refused (`exists`).
  - **T023 binding**: `add_fact(rights, fact)` stores a reported fact as a primary source with one whole-record passage (resolved instrument, the fact's own feed and receipt time); the same fact from the same feed again returns the existing passage.
- **`tests/test_evidence.py` (new)**, 52 cases including the property. **`tests/ingest_rights_helper.py`**: `qualified_registry(feed_ids)` extracted to qualify two feeds in one registry; existing tests unchanged and passing.

## Negative and adverse cases
- Bad citations: wrong entity, attribute, period (other year; instant vs duration), unit, value; each refused with its code and not stored. `100.00` matches `100`.
- Passages: wrong offset, out-of-range span, empty quote; reading value 999 against "Revenues were 100 USD" refused (the review probe; the claim stays unsupported), 2025 against "FY2025", 100 against "1000", 1000 against "1,0000", 100 against "100A7" and "1,00" refused; "1,000" vs 1000, "1000" vs 1000.00 and "-12.5" accepted. Per separator (space, NBSP, U+2009, U+202F, apostrophe): `1<sep>000` refuses 1 and accepts 1000; `12 345.67` refuses 12 and 345.67 and accepts 12345.67; `1 00` refuses 1 and 100; `2025 100` refuses 100; `1,000 000` refuses 1000 and 1000000.
- Publication: a source published 30 days after receipt cannot be cited before then and is supported from its publication instant; a T023 fact received before its filed-date-bound publication time cannot be cited until that time.
- Re-ingest: identical content keeps the first receipt time; other content or another feed under the same id is refused.
- Syndication: the same release from two feeds (re-typeset) counts once; a declared copy with other wording counts once; a genuinely different source makes it corroborated.
- Withdrawal of the original invalidates its syndicated copy at that time, the claim stays in history, and new citations to the copy are refused. Deleting one copy leaves the other usable and flags `replay_limited`.
- Retention: supported one microsecond before expiry, `retention_expired` at it; purge drops text. Revocation: entitlement deactivated at R: supported before, `rights_revoked` from R.
- Ingest without a retention grant is denied; a tenant without entitlement cannot add sources.
- Revisions: old citation `revision_superseded`, as-of before the revision still supported; backdated revision and kind change refused.
- Knowledge time: backdated mutation, claim not yet known, naive datetime, integer value.
- Tenant isolation: another tenant cannot cite, read support, add passages, withdraw or read text; same claim id in another tenant cannot cite this tenant's passage.
- **Mutation controls (self-report, scratchpad, source restored and checked with `sha256sum -c`).** 25/25 killed by the suite after review round 2: value-in-quote check off, word lookbehind off, digit-group lookbehind off, digit-group lookahead off, word lookahead off, inconsistent separators allowed (survived until the `1,000 000` vs 1000000 case was added), separators not removed, publication time ignored, re-ingest resetting receipt, withdrawal per copy, origin not normalized, revision binding off, retention ignored, deletion ignored, rights not rechecked, unit/entity/period unchecked, link time ignored, unusable source citable, social citable, backdating allowed, copies counted as corroboration, span unchecked, tenant ignored on lookup. Before the fixes, against the property alone: withdrawal, retention, deletion and revision mutants killed in 3 of 3 runs after retuning, origin normalization in 2 of 3; link-time and corroboration mutants killed in the one run before retuning (not re-measured).

## Interpretations and gaps
- "Non-duplicate-only" is read as: a syndicated copy cannot stand in for withdrawn content (withdrawal covers the origin group), and copies never add corroboration. Deletion and rights act per copy.
- Origin groups are content origin, not publisher independence: two different documents from one publisher or feed are two groups and can corroborate each other. Publisher-level independence is not modelled.
- Origin grouping by normalized text catches exact re-publication only; near-duplicate detection must be declared through `same_origin_as` by a later dedupe step.
- Support reads rights as known at `at`; serving a claim now must pass the current time (T021 precondition).
- The quote check reads only the number: unit, period and scale words ("million", "(100)" accounting negatives) are not parsed from the text; they rest on the structured `Reading`, bound to the passage hash. A quote stating the value in thousands with the unit elsewhere cannot be verified and is refused unless the plain value appears.
- `not_yet_published` in `support` is defensive: `cite` already refuses before publication and support counts only citations linked by `at`.
- (Superseded by increment 2 below.) Contradictions were not detected in increment 1: two claims with one key and different values can both read `supported` until increment 2 lands (draft in the orchestrator scratchpad). Calculation, assumption, forecast and interpretation claims are refused (`kind_unsupported`).
- **T027 binding: not done.** `strategy_gate.EvidenceRecord` refs are qualification artifacts keyed by material/config hash and tenant (backtest and experiment receipts, T032), not source passages; no natural mapping exists yet.
- `add_fact` does not compare the fact's stored feed hash with the current feed revision.

# Increment 2: contradictions and resolution

Status: In progress. Increment 2 of T036 adds contradiction detection and explicit resolution on top of the merged increment 1 (`909aff8`). Branch `t036-contradictions`. Persistence and routes remain. Backlog status is unchanged. The worker did not commit.

## Scope
Ported from the increment-1 draft that was moved out at review, onto the merged module (publication gate, quote value check, idempotent ingest). Requirement IDs: R085 (structured, attributable resolution record, no model reasoning needed) and R071 (as-of evaluation; resolutions never rewrite history). It also covers the §10 "contrary evidence" step that claim verification needs.

## Changes
- **`qw_domain/evidence.py`**
  - `Status.CONTESTED`, `ResolutionState` (`unresolved`, `resolved`), `Resolution` (tenant, member set, preferred claim, actor id, time) and `Contradiction` (key, members, values, state, matching resolution).
  - `support` now delegates citation checks to `_check`.
  - **Membership**: evidence-supported current revisions only. An unsupported claim neither contradicts nor is contested.
  - **Contradiction**: members share a key (entity, attribute, period, unit) and hold two or more distinct values as of `at`. Members are (claim id, revision) pairs.
  - **Resolution**: a contradiction is `resolved` only by the latest resolution recorded by `at` whose member set equals the current one. A new member, a new revision, or a member losing support (withdrawal, expiry, revocation, revision) changes the set and reopens it.
  - **Status under a resolution**: members holding the preferred claim's value read `supported`; the others stay `contested`.
  - `resolve(tenant, members, preferred, actor, at, rights)` refuses a stale member set (`stale_contradiction`), a non-member preference (`preferred`), a malformed actor id and a backdated time (tenant clock). Records are append-only; `resolutions(tenant)` returns them.
  - `contradictions(tenant, at, rights)` is evaluated as of `at`.
- **`tests/test_evidence_contradictions.py` (new)**: 4 cases and a Hypothesis property.

**Property** (300 examples): 2 to 4 claims on one key, each with a value, cited or not, and a source that may be withdrawn. Claim 0 may be revised (uncited) at hour 6, and a resolution with a drawn preferred member is attempted at hour 4. The oracle, computed from the drawn parameters:
- A resolution attempt must be refused (`stale_contradiction`) when the claims supported at hour 4 do not disagree.
- At the query time, the key is contested iff the supported claims hold two or more distinct values and the resolution recorded by then has exactly the current member set.
- Every claim's status matches: unsupported, contested, or supported (when it agrees with the preferred value or there is no contradiction).

Observed in a scratch instrumented copy, over two runs: 41-42 contested, 24-52 resolved and the rest with no contradiction.

## Negative and adverse cases
- An agreeing claim joins the member set; a claim on another unit is another key; an unsupported claim with a different value is ignored.
- As of a time before the disagreeing claim, or before the resolution, the earlier status holds.
- Resolution refusals: stale member set, preferred claim not a member, blank actor, backdated time.
- A later resolution supersedes an earlier one from its time on.
- Reopening: by a new member, by a cited revision, and when a member loses support through withdrawal.
- Tenant isolation: the same key in another tenant does not contradict; another tenant cannot resolve this tenant's member set.
- **Mutation controls (self-report, scratchpad, source restored and checked with `sha256sum -c`)**: 12/12 killed by the suite:
  - membership not restricted to supported claims;
  - resolution not bound to the member set;
  - resolution as-of ignored;
  - earliest resolution winning;
  - contested status ignored;
  - losing members not contested;
  - stale check off;
  - preferred claim unchecked;
  - actor unchecked;
  - resolution backdating allowed;
  - key ignoring the unit;
  - tenant ignored.

  The property alone kills the first three, contested-ignored, losers-not-contested and stale-check-off.

## Interpretations and gaps
- The task spec gives no rule for rejected values. Members whose value differs from the preferred one stay `contested` after resolution rather than becoming `unsupported`, because their evidence is still valid.
- There is no dismissal state ("not a real contradiction"). The owner should decide whether one is wanted.
- A resolution whose member set recurs (identical claim ids and revisions) applies again. Member sets only change through new claims, revisions or lost support, so a set recurs only when a member regains support.
- Contradictions compare claims. Contrary evidence on passages without a claim is not detected.
- Each `support` call recomputes the tenant's contradictions (quadratic). Persistence should index by key.
