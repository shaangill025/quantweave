### Transition rules
- connected needs `auth_tested` evidence; qualified needs `workload_tested` **and** `permission_granted` evidence recorded at or after the bound revision's `recorded_at` (so old evidence cannot requalify a revised feed), plus every required use, a scope and a jurisdiction granted and unexpired at the transition time; suspended and retired need at least one evidence record. Evidence recorded after the transition time is ignored. Revisions and transitions are recorded in time order. Retired is terminal.
- QUALIFIED → QUALIFIED (requalification after a revision) is an addition to the §16 chain, which names no explicit requalification step.

### Integrity guard
`Registry` stores `MappingProxyType` copies of its maps (outside mutation of the passed dicts has no effect) and, on every construction, replays each feed and entitlement history from scratch through `revise`/`transition` and requires the result to equal the supplied history. This re-derives every content hash, numbers revisions 1..n and re-applies transition legality, actor, evidence and time-order rules, including for a brand-new feed or entitlement; a forged `qualified` transition with no evidence is refused (`integrity`). Map keys must match record ids; feeds need a registered provider, entitlements a registered feed. `with_*` additionally refuse anything other than extending an existing history. Cost: every `with_*` replays the whole registry (fine for the domain; persistence will verify per write).
- Qualification binds (revision, hash). Any later rights revision, even one restoring earlier content, revokes it until a new `qualified` transition (§6: policy changes revoke dependent qualification).

### `check_use(registry, tenant, feed, use, at, *, scope, jurisdiction, retain_for=None)`
`at` must be aware (naive raises `InstantError`), normalized to UTC. **Precondition: `at` is the time of use.** The gate reads the feed revision, feed status, evidence and entitlement revision known at `at` (a revision recorded after `at` neither allows nor denies retroactively), so the caller controls what is "known"; the persistence/API layer must set `at` from the server clock and never accept it from a client. Deny reasons (`DenyCode`), all collected: `right_unknown`, `right_denied`, `right_expired`, `not_qualified` (unregistered feed, status other than qualified **as known at `at`**, rights revised, or use outside the qualified set), `not_entitled` (no or inactive entitlement for this tenant), `feed_retired`, `cross_tenant_pooling_not_granted` (needs grants in both profiles, an installation licence and a consent ref; a user's own credential never pools), `retention_exceeded` (duration missing or above a grant). Both the feed profile (installation) and the entitlement profile (tenant) must grant the use, scope and jurisdiction. The decision returns the feed and entitlement revision hashes for downstream lineage.

### Scoping decision for persistence
Providers, feeds and feed rights are installation-scoped reference records (spec §3 separates global reference data from tenant data; data dictionary `feed_spec` retention class `reference`). Entitlements are tenant-scoped (data dictionary `entitlement`, scope `tenant`) and need tenant RLS.

## Global versus tenant data (spec §3, ADR-004): decision
| Table | Scope | Read | Write |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rights_provider`, `rights_feed`, `rights_feed_revision`, `rights_feed_transition` | installation (global provider and feed facts, data dictionary `feed_spec` class `reference`) | `qw_app`, `qw_worker` (SELECT) | schema owner `qw_migrate` only |
| `rights_entitlement` | tenant (data dictionary `entitlement`) | `qw_app`, `qw_worker` (SELECT) under forced tenant RLS | `qw_migrate` only, inside a tenant transaction (still subject to the forced tenant policy) |

- Every table keeps FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY, so the catalogue invariant in `test_tenancy` still holds. The global tables have an explicit open `FOR SELECT USING (true)` policy and an `owner_insert` policy `FOR INSERT TO qw_migrate`. No runtime role holds INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE, so a compromised API or worker process cannot grant, extend or revoke a right.
- The privileged writer is the operator-held schema owner. Migrations cannot create a dedicated registry role, because the runner refuses role changes outside 0001 and `qw_migrate` is NOCREATEROLE. T008 §5 describes `qw_migrate` as deploy-time only, so recording rights at runtime needs either an owner decision or a new bootstrap role (see remaining work).

## Tests and negative cases (SYNTHETIC; the reads use the non-superuser `qw_app`/`qw_worker` logins)
- An empty store loads as `Registry()` and denies display with `not_qualified`. Every rights table has 0 rows after migration.
- Round trip: two qualified feeds and an entitlement missing `redistribution` reload equal to the in-memory registry as both `qw_app` and `qw_worker`. Recording the same records again adds nothing. The missing use is still denied (`entitlement:use:redistribution`).
- Tenant isolation: tenant B sees the global feeds but no entitlement (`not_entitled`) and counts 0 entitlement rows. Writing A's entitlement from B's transaction raises `tenant`, and a raw owner insert of it is refused by row-level security.
- Authority: `qw_app` and `qw_worker` cannot record a provider or an entitlement (`database`). They cannot UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE any rights table (InsufficientPrivilege), and the owner's DELETE is refused by the append-only trigger.
- After reload, display is allowed one second before a grant's expiry and `right_expired` at it. An inactive entitlement revision gives `not_entitled`, and a `suspended` transition gives `not_qualified`. Uses at a time before the revocation keep what was known then. Another tenant's entitlement is unaffected, and the suspension is a third transition row.
- Refused writes: a history that does not extend the stored one, and a renamed provider (`append_only`). A feed of an unregistered provider (`provider_unregistered`). A knowledge time one hour old, or one hour in the future, is refused by the database trigger.
- Window boundary: a feed revision 50 seconds old is accepted. One 70 seconds old, or 10 seconds in the future, is refused.
- Previous row: raw owner inserts dated 10 seconds before the feed's last row (a transition or a revision), or before the entitlement's last revision, are refused (`check_violation`, "previous row").
- Tenant filter: a superuser connection, which bypasses RLS, loads tenant A's registry without tenant B's stored entitlement, and the result equals the `qw_app` load. The query filter holds without RLS.
- Lock: while a reader's transaction is open it holds the advisory `ShareLock` (checked in `pg_locks`). An owner writer with `lock_timeout` 300 ms fails (`database`), then succeeds once the reader has finished.
- 12 superuser tamper cases each fail load with `integrity`: grant flipped to denied, content hash replaced, expiry extended, entitlement silently granted display, entitlement source changed, a transition deleted, qualified evidence emptied, connected rewritten to qualified, duplicated required uses, feed moved to another provider, and two equal-meaning non-canonical forms (reordered uses, `+00:00` offsets).
- The owner inserts a `qualified` transition with no evidence past the adapter. Load fails closed.
- API: on an empty DB the existing test still serves `restricted:not_qualified` through the default store. A new test records a SYNTHETIC qualified feed and entitlement for tenant A, and the evidence route serves the text while the claim is `supported`. Tenant B gets `restricted:not_entitled`. After an inactive revision is recorded, the next request is `restricted`. A tampered entitlement row returns a 500 without the text.

## Unresolved gates and remaining T021 work
1. T004 and T002: no provider has qualified rights evidence, so nothing may be recorded for a real install until the owner records it.
2. Writer role, pending owner decision: operator tooling either records rights as `qw_migrate` or uses a new bootstrap role (for example `qw_registry`, which needs a change to the role bootstrap). Whether tenant-owned BYO-credential entitlements become writable by `qw_app` is also undecided.
3. The 60-second back-dating window is an owner design gate, not a spec number.
6. Not authenticated: `actor_id`, evidence `ref_id`s and `recorded_at` within the window are whatever the privileged writer supplies. `recorded_at` and transition fields are not covered by the content hash, which covers the rights body only. Only replay rules and the database-clock window constrain them.
7. Out-of-order owner insert (runbook): the tables are append-only, so a raw owner insert that the domain would refuse (for example a transition out of order or without evidence) makes `load` fail for the whole installation and every route returns 500. This fails closed. Repair means the owner disabling the triggers (`session_replication_role = replica`) to remove the row, as a recorded operator action.
8. Lock DoS: load takes a global shared advisory lock (`hashtextextended('rights', 0)`) and writers an exclusive one. A compromised runtime process could take that lock exclusively and stall every rights load or write, the same class of risk as the existing evidence and ledger advisory locks. Lock order is evidence before rights; rights writers take no other lock.
9. Scale: every request loads and replays the whole registry (all feeds, revisions and transitions, plus this tenant's entitlements). That is fine at personal-pilot size and will not scale; a cached, version-checked snapshot is future work.
10. Operator rule: the global tables (`rights_provider`, `rights_feed*`) must never hold tenant-specific data. Every runtime role in every tenant can read them. Anything tenant-specific belongs in `rights_entitlement`.
4. Nits from increment 1 remain: evidence `ref_id`s are not resolved against an evidence store, and pooling consent is an id only.
5. Still to build: quotas, latency mode and allowed instruments (§6); the `/capabilities` read model; reassessment fan-out on revision; the rights-exclusion report. Backlog status not changed.
