## PR A — lifecycle
Files: `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/proposals.py` (526 lines), `packages/domain/tests/test_proposals.py` (408 lines; 12 tests, 21 cases), and this receipt. PR A does not import `commitments.py`.
- **States** are the contract's eleven. Execution tracking is orthogonal; only `planned` is set here. `TRANSITIONS` is a table, as in `jobs.py`. Every live state can expire, be invalidated or be superseded. Only `publish` enters `active`.
- **Versions** are immutable and carry a SHA-256 content hash. The `Binding` holds:
  - the adopted policy hash;
  - account journal revisions;
  - observation times with a maximum age per input;
  - a risk digest: outcome, reasons, requested size, policy hash and method. Limit values and the feasible size are left out on purpose, so a price tick alone is not material.

  F-15 time order is enforced: trigger ≤ received ≤ created < expires. History is an append-only event list.
- **Mode** comes from the adopted policy's `ai_mode` (ONB16). `Proposal.new` and `revise` refuse a version whose mode differs.
- **Final check.** `Current` carries the time, the policy history, the risk inputs, the account revisions and the pauses. `final_check` recomputes the risk evaluation from them, so a digest cannot be supplied separately. The version is invalidated for any of:
  - a stale input (per key);
  - a policy hash that changed or is missing;
  - an account revision that changed or is missing;
  - a covering pause (buys, and sales of stock that covers a short call);
  - a changed risk digest;
  - a tenant mismatch.

  It expires at or after `expires_at`. Every decision settles first. A late decision is refused and recorded, never applied. Times never go back.
- **Revise** keeps the tenant, account and currency, and the mode must be the policy's. It settles first and refuses a closed version. **A planned version is not revised** (`ALREADY_DECIDED`): the user's plan is bound to the content they accepted. §13 requires a new version for material changes, so a changed planned proposal is invalidated by the final check and replaced by a new proposal. Before activation, one revision is allowed (AT074, `REVISION_LIMIT`).
- **Decisions.**
  - `publish` takes an `Admit` callback for the commitment check. A refusal leaves the version `ready_for_final_check`, and a bound BLOCK outcome rejects it.
  - `mark_planned` is the user's accept. Siblings must be keyed by their own id, be in the same group and share the tenant, account and currency (`sibling_key`, `sibling_group`, `group_scope`). If any sibling was ever planned, the accept is refused (`ALTERNATIVE_SELECTED`), and a planned sibling is never invalidated. Otherwise the active siblings are invalidated.
  - `dismiss` closes an active version.

## PR B (stacked) — planning commitments
Files: `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/commitments.py` (283 lines), `packages/domain/tests/test_commitments.py` (484 lines; 14 tests, 27 cases).
- `CommitmentBook` is keyed by (tenant, account, currency). Its available cash is qualified cash and excludes unexecuted sales, transfers and deposits.
- `publish_committed` passes `book.admit` as the callback. Admission applies every other commitment to cash, allocation usage, held units, position values and issuer and sector exposure, then re-runs `risk.evaluate`, so concentration and stress limits see the joint set. No risk maths is duplicated.
  - Members of other alternatives groups all count toward exposure; this is conservative.
  - Other sales never reduce exposure, but they reduce held units when a sale is evaluated, so two sales cannot jointly oversell.
  - **Worst case per check.** Admission evaluates two states and both must pass. The pending state (cash, allocation, concentration) counts every other buy as executed and no other sale. The stress state fixes each other commitment independently, because stress is linear in position values and each commitment fills or not: a buy counts as executed only if its shock is ≤ 0 (a pending hedge buy may never fill); a sale only if its shock is > 0 (removing a hedge raises the loss); an instrument without a shock stays in, so `risk.evaluate` blocks `STRESS_SHOCK_MISSING`. The stress state is evaluated only when it differs, and a refusal from it is `RISK_BLOCKED` with detail `stress_worst_case:…`. For an executed hedge sale the removed value is max(quantity × current or commitment mark, the listed value's share of the units sold), exact and rounded up.
  - Reserved cash must stay within `available`: alternatives count their maximum, everything else adds. Admission is first come, first served, so the admitted set is maximal but depends on order.
  - A buy reserves max(bound requirement, cost at the current ask or last mark).
  - If the book and the inputs disagree on cash, admission returns a typed `CASH_UNCONFIRMED` refusal.
- **Groups** live in one book. A group with a planned member refuses new members. `plan_committed` requires every other committed member as a sibling, marks the commitment planned and releases the siblings.
- `sync` keeps only the commitment of an active latest version. This releases on supersede, expiry, invalidation, rejection and dismissal. Callers pass every lifecycle decision through `sync`; the B functions sync what they return.

## Review
- Reviewer: an independent subagent running a different model from the implementer, two rounds; it ran every check itself, on the full tree and on PR A alone, plus its own adversarial scripts.
- Round 1 (single PR): CHANGES REQUESTED. Blocking: commitments leaked when a revised version expired or was rejected; a late sibling could invalidate an already planned alternative; joint feasibility covered cash and allocation only, so two individually valid buys could together breach issuer concentration or stress. Should-fix: `revise` could change mode, account or tenant and skip review; revise did not settle first; the reservation used a stale mark; the risk digest was caller-supplied; alternatives groups were unscoped across books; the receipt overstated stale mark/FX coverage. The work was then split into PR A (lifecycle) and PR B (commitments).
- Round 2: PR A approved, with every round-1 finding fixed. The reviewer's randomized harness (3000 seeds × 40 operations over five proposals in two groups) found no leaked, duplicated or over-reserved commitment, no lost planned decision and no invalidated planned sibling. PR B: one new should-fix, N1 (a pending sale of a positive-shock hedge could let a joint buy breach stress).
- Round 3 (PR B): N1 fixed; new should-fix N2 (executing all pending sales let a negative-shock sale's relief hide a hedge sale's harm).
- Round 4 (PR B): N2 fixed; new should-fix N3, the mirror case (a pending hedge buy was counted on).
- Round 5 (PR B): APPROVE. N3 fixed by the per-commitment worst-case rule. The reviewer's own randomized harness (scratchpad, not in the repository) admitted 1500 random sets of 2–5 mixed buys and sells with mixed shock signs and checked every subset of fills against `risk.evaluate`: 4728 subset checks, 0 stress violations; with the fix disabled the same harness found 4 violations, so it has power. Partial fills are covered by linearity.
- Nit carried to increment 2: `step`, `revise`, `dismiss` and `settle` rely on the caller syncing the book; the publisher should centralise this.

# Increment 2 — PostgreSQL store and atomic publication

Status: In progress. Branch `t026-proposal-store`, base `d138646`; not committed. Requirement IDs: R020, R049, R067, R082, R083, R085, R086. T008: F-09, F-10 and the lock order; C-04 only as a typed 409 result (no route yet).

## Changed files
- `sql/0010_proposals.sql`:
  - `proposal_version` and `proposal_event` are append-only. `proposal_commitment` goes held → planned → released, is never deleted, and has at most one live row per proposal.
  - FORCE RLS, tenant FKs, column grants to `qw_app` and `qw_worker`.
  - The event trigger mirrors `TRANSITIONS` (`app.proposal_step_ok`), with a gapless sequence and a new version only after supersede. A commitment is inserted or planned only while the latest event is that version, active, with the matching execution. Version columns must agree with the content.
  - **The trigger bounds `at` only to [previous `at`, now]. The server-clock decision time is chosen by the adapter.**
  - **The database does not check capital.** A raw `qw_app` insert can write any `reserved_wire`. Capital is enforced by the adapter under the account lock.
- `proposal_store.py`: `ProposalPublisher(tx)` with `submit`, `step`, `revise`, `publish`, `decide` (plan or dismiss), `expire_due` and `load`. Every transition goes through `_apply`, which centralises sync (the increment-1 nit):
  - It locks the sorted accounts named by the version and its binding (the journal's key), then the policy.
  - It sweeps expired commitments in the book.
  - It re-reads `journal_store.revision` and `policy_store.load`, and takes the time from `clock_timestamp()`.
  - It runs the domain function, then writes versions, events (one outbox row each via `jobs.write_outbox`) and the commitment difference in the caller's transaction.
  - A refusal is `Result.status == 409` with typed reasons. Reads recompute content hashes.
  - **A mid-`_apply` error leaves the transaction unusable: the caller must roll back, never commit.**
- **Locks, revised after review.** The book (account, currency) and sleeve advisory locks were removed as redundant. Every writer of a book or sleeve holds that account's lock, so the account lock covers the currency and sleeve classes of the T008 order. The order is now accounts (sorted), then the policy; it is deadlock-free.
- `qw_domain/proposals.py`: `ProposalVersion.canonical`, and `expire(p, as_of)`, an input-free expiry equal to what `settle` records.
- Tests: `test_proposal_store.py` (7 database tests), `test_proposals.py` (`expire` equals `settle`), `test_tenancy.py` (the new tables are in the forced-RLS list).
- **Caller-built inputs.** Risk inputs and pauses come from the caller (`Snapshot`); the book's cash is the snapshot's `available_cash`. The store itself re-reads the revision and the policy.
- **Precondition for the future `/proposals` routes:** `ProposalPublisher` must never be reachable with client-supplied `Snapshot` inputs, and `available_cash` must come server-side from T017.

## Database tests (PostgreSQL 16, non-superuser `qw_app`; SYNTHETIC)
- **F-09 / NUM06.** Two valid 4000 buys against 5000, from two threads on separate connections, over 12 rounds:
  - Even rounds are a free race behind a barrier.
  - Odd rounds hold the first transaction open until the second waits on an advisory lock (`pg_locks`).
  - In rounds 2–3, 6–7 and 10–11 the second proposal is under a second adopted policy, so only the account lock serialises them.
  - Each round: exactly one is accepted; the other gets 409 with `CAPITAL_CONFLICT` and `RISK_BLOCKED`; the live total is 4000; a dismissal then releases it.
- **Atomicity.** An exception after `publish` inside the transaction leaves no event, outbox row or commitment. A real publish adds exactly one of each (payload checked), and `load` round-trips. A `revise` supersedes version 1 and releases the commitment.
- **F-10.** After a journal deposit: 409 `ACCOUNT_CHANGED`, the version is invalidated, nothing is reserved. After a new policy adoption: `POLICY_CHANGED` as well.
- **Alternatives.** Both members publish (the group reserves its maximum); an ungrouped 1500 buy is refused. Planning one member invalidates and releases the other.
- **Late decision.** After expiry, a plan is refused with exactly `EXPIRED` and the commitment is released; the other expired holder is swept. `expire_due` returns `["e"]`, then `[]`, with 3 outbox rows in total.
- **Tenancy.** Another tenant sees no rows and gets `None` from `load`, `not_found` from `publish`, and a refusal for a version naming another tenant.
- **Triggers.**
  - `proposal_step_ok` equals the domain table on all 121 pairs.
  - Refused: an illegal step; an execution change; a sequence gap; a version without supersede; a commitment for a non-active version; an event update; planning without a planned event; an amount change; released → held; delete.
  - A tampered version fails `load` with `integrity`.
