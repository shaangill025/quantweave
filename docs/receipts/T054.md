## Changes
| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `apps/web/src/sources.ts` (new) | `ResearchSource` interface and `httpSource`. The source calls only contract routes: claim, evidence, calculation, option terms and the simulations list. Ids are checked against the contract pattern before any path is built. The option scenario grid and the differentiated scorecard have **no contract route or schema**. For these two, `httpSource` returns `no_contract_route` and sends no request. Their typed shapes follow `qw_domain.option_scenarios`, `option_greeks` and `scorecard`. Runtime guards cover all seven shapes. Decimals must pass `isDecimalString`. A simulation needs `separate_from_actual: true`. A scorecard row's origin must allow its record kind (user: actual or virtual; AI: virtual or recommendation; benchmark: baseline only). Its currency must match the plan, and a missing result must carry its reason. Also adds `termBlockers`, `quoteBlockers` and `percent`. `percent` moves the decimal point in the string, with no number conversion. |
| `apps/web/src/ResearchViews.tsx` (new) | **Research.** The evidence drilldown opens a claim by id. It shows kind and verification as text tags, materiality, valid-at time, and supporting sources, contrary evidence and calculations as separate lists. Each source shows locator, rights profile, availability limitation, passage or `Unavailable (availability)`, and published and retrieved times in UTC. A missing publication time is shown as Unavailable. Each calculation shows formula and version, status, result or `Unavailable (status)`, inputs with unit and source, and warnings. **Option scenarios.** The contract terms table shows a missing multiplier or deliverables as Unavailable. Any term blocker, or a quote that is stale, unknown or not rights-permitted, gives "[Scenarios blocked] reasons". Payoffs and Greeks are then not requested or shown. Otherwise the grid shows exact expiry rows apart from model-estimate rows, gap rows with their reason, a model-applicability tag, Greeks with server-supplied units, and worst loss and collateral (separate from premium P&L), each Unavailable when absent. **Simulation.** A list of virtual portfolios with mode and execution model. "Work with X in simulation mode" selects one. The scorecard shows separate User decisions, AI recommendations and Benchmarks tables, with no total row. Each row shows a record tag ([Real account], [SIMULATED], [Recommendation record], [Benchmark]), its sample size, "[Insufficient evidence]" (result hidden) when below the predeclared minimum or when no minimum exists, a percentage with an explicit interval, or "No interval: uncertainty not estimated". |

The `role=status` "Mode" banner reads "[SIMULATED] Simulation mode: X…" with "Leave simulation mode", or "Mode: real-account analysis." The document title gets the "[SIMULATED]" prefix. The broker note now reads "Analysis only…", because "Real-account analysis only" would be false in simulation mode. This supersedes the banner wording recorded in the T053 receipt.
