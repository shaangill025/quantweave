### Rules
- **Search space (§11 "permitted mutations").** A `SearchSpace` names one registered strategy version (id, version, material hash), the parameters a candidate may change with bounds inside the manifest's (numeric range or a subset of choices, typed, never float), a research-trial budget `max_trials` and a floor `min_prospective_scored` for shadow runs. Its hash must equal the T044 protocol's `mutation_space_hash` (`search_space_not_frozen`); re-registering the protocol with a wider space is `protocol_frozen` naming the field. Dimension names containing a risk, policy, threshold, approval, holdout, budget, limit, scorecard, assessor or consent token are refused (`protected_dimension`).
- **Candidate = derived version, not code.** `derive` copies the base manifest and changes only the defaults of declared dimensions; anything else (another key, out of the space, a float, no change) is refused. `check_derived` re-derives from the diff and requires the same material hash, so a changed implementation hash, session flag or parameter bound is `outside_search_space`. The T044 `Candidate` carries the derived material hash as its artifact and the resolved defaults as its payload, so its trial-ledger key equals what `shadow.open_run` derives for that version with `{}` configuration. The same defaults under a new id are refused by T044 as `identical_artifact`.
- **Trial accounting (R029, R084, §14).** Trials use the T032 family named by the experiment id; everything on the protocol's plan or in that family counts (round 1). `request_research_access` refuses the holdout (`assessor_only`), checks the plan and its frozen backtest protocol, and refuses a new trial id once `max_trials` research trials (recorded in any outcome, or granted and unrecorded) exist (`trial_budget_exhausted`). `account` returns the ledger report (every trial, any outcome), the research count, unrecorded accesses and trials of variants never registered as candidates; violations are `hard:unrecorded_trials`, `trial_budget_exceeded`, `unregistered_variant_trials`, `foreign_family_trials`.
- **Development evaluation.** `develop` checks that the access was granted for this candidate and that the strategy registry holds its derived version, then runs T033 `run_trial` with the version's resolved defaults (the trial is recorded even if it fails).
- **Assessment.** `assess_strategy` requires the study's protocol to be the registered one, runs T044 `assess` (scorecard with the three strategy hard controls, holdout read once by the assessor's own actor), then:
  1. T044 blocked stays blocked;
  2. a T044 rejection or any accounting violation rejects;
  3. otherwise a prospective window is required: a shadow run for this candidate's version and ledger key, under the study's backtest protocol, registered after the protocol freeze and the candidate, declaring at least the study's minimum, and scored now by the assessor from the captured book with the declaration hash recorded at registration (`shadow.score`). A missing, foreign, early, under-declared, invalid or below-minimum window gives **insufficient evidence**, i.e. research only.
  4. Only then can the decision be accept-for-review. The ceiling is the shadow report's, capped at `limited` when the holdout plan is synthetic; it is `none` otherwise. `significance_claimed` is always false; `no_multiple_testing_adjustment` is always a limitation.
- **Adoption artifact (R034, R099).** `adoption_candidate` exists only for an accept-for-review assessment whose T044 verdict is recorded in the registry (`verdict_not_recorded`) and whose trial accounting is unchanged (`trials_changed`). It bundles lineage, base and candidate versions and hashes, the parameter diff, protocol, search space, backtest protocol, risk policy hash, scorecard, every trial row, the verdict, the prospective report, the ceiling and limitations, the required approvals (maintainer release, operator install, user strategy adoption) and `granted_approvals: []`, `installs: false`, `adopts: false`, plus a content hash. Nothing in the module adopts, installs, approves, reserves budget or records a verdict (AST test).
- **Risk and policy.** A candidate can only change declared strategy defaults; the risk policy hash and scorecard stay frozen in the protocol, and the artifact reports them unchanged.
