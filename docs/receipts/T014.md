## PR A0 — journal holding ledger (`journal.py`, `test_journal.py`)
- **New on `Journal`:** `journal_id`, `copy()` and `revision(account)` (a per-account counter).
- **Holding ledger:** a per-(account, instrument) ledger of cumulative units and cost in effective-time order.
  - `holding()` and the SELL guard bisect it instead of folding the journal.
  - A back-dated event updates the later sums.
  - `_check` no longer copies the key index.
- **N2, mixed cost currencies:**
  - `holding()`, the SELL guard and `Journal.sell` now raise `JournalError("cost_currency_mixed")` for a holding whose cost was ever posted in two currencies.
  - `materialize` cannot total such a holding either; it raises `CurrencyMismatchError`.
- **Tests (R1):**
  - Derandomized Hypothesis differential property (200 examples, up to 25 operations each) with `materialize` as the oracle. Operations: back-dated buys, sells, raw sells from stale snapshots (one unit over, exactly all, or any), splits, reversals and duplicate replays.
  - After each step, `holding()` must equal the oracle with and without own-record exclusion at three times. The guard must refuse exactly when `materialize` of the events plus the new sale goes negative.
  - Copies are deep and get a new id; revision equals 1 + the number of entries.
  - Also: a mixed-currency test, and a corrected sale whose pending reversal the guard counts.
- **Mutation controls** (each alone, all caught): back-dated update off; own-record exclusion off; guard off; guard off by one; guard too strict; pending reversal ignored; wrong bisection; insertion at the end; mixed-currency check off; shallow ledger copy.
- **Notes for the persistence increment:**
  - N3: `journal_id` and revisions are in-memory and do not survive a restart, and the revision check and the posts are not under one lock. Persistence must bind a stored journal identity and lock the account revisions for the commit.
  - N4: a back-dated insert costs O(later entries of that holding). A preview inserts in effective-time order, so only events already in the journal pay this cost.

## PR A1 — transaction files (`csv_import.py`, `test_csv_import.py`, this receipt)
- **Format `canonical-csv/1`:** the 11 fixture columns plus optional `fee_currency`. When `fee_currency` is unmapped, the fee is assumed to be in the trade currency.
- **File rejection:** bytes, NUL, strict UTF-8 (BOM removed), strict `csv`, rows, columns, header-cell length, duplicate header, missing mapped column, format version. `csv.field_size_limit` is process-global and stays at its default (131072).
- **Mapping:** a versioned `ColumnMapping`. Unmapped headers stay raw in `unknown_fields`, are never screened, and must go through `spreadsheet_safe` on export.
- **Row outcomes:** accepted, duplicate, conflict, or quarantined with one of 18 `Reason` codes.
  - The formula check (`= + - @ TAB CR`) covers mapped text fields only.
  - A quarantined row's record id and unknown fields are cut to 64 characters and neutralized.
  - A zero price is `field_invalid`; a sale of a mixed-currency holding is `mixed_currency`.
  - Rows run in effective-time order on `Journal.copy()`; a preview never mutates the journal.
- **`commit(preview, journal, recorded_at, tenant_id, log)` never raises.**
  - Rejections: `tenant_mismatch` (N1), `journal_mismatch`, an unvalidated preview, or a `JournalError` code such as `recorded_at_order`.
  - A changed account revision is a `conflict`.
  - Events are trial-posted on a copy first.
  - Only accepted rows are observed, so `50.0` versus `50` adds no log conflict.
  - Gap: `SourceMap` and `SecurityMaster` changes after the preview are not bound.
- **Provenance:** import id, SHA-256 file hash, mapping version, and the `SourceRef` on each accepted event and observation.
- **Tests:** 59 SYNTHETIC tests in `test_csv_import.py`.
  - NUM01 from the fixture against `numerical_oracles.json`.
  - Re-import no-op, changed-row conflict, and a duplicate written differently.
  - Binding with distinct reasons; back-dated rows; BOM and CRLF.
  - 29 row-reason cases (1 control); unknown cost; mixed fee and cost currencies; injection; raw unknown fields and retention.
  - 11 file-rejection cases (1 control); format version; mapping.
  - Hypothesis shuffle property (derandomized, 60 examples).
- **Mutation controls** (each alone, all caught): duplicates observed; binding off; commit raising; zero price allowed; unknown fields screened; retention off; plus the 13 controls from round 1.
- **Preview cost** (SYNTHETIC, one account, before tenant/mixed-currency changes):
  - before the ledger, alternating buys and sells grew quadratically: 1,000 rows took 12.04 s;
  - after: 10,000 rows took 1.65 s (buys only) and 1.87 s (alternating);
  - 50,000 rows took 9.42 s and 12.00 s;
  - the defaults are therefore `max_rows` 10,000 and `max_bytes` 2 MiB.

## Increment 2 (persistence and commit)
Branch `t014-import-commit-store`, base `23c0ab7`. No new dependencies, no network. The worker did not commit. Resolves N3: the stored identity is the tenant plus each account's `account_rev`, and the revision check and the posts run under the same account locks in one transaction.

### Changes
- `sql/0008_import_preview.sql` (new): table `app.import_preview`.
  - Columns: `preview_id` (uuid), tenant, the uploaded bytes (≤ 8 MiB; NULL once redacted) and the request (format, mapping, limits, MIC). Also:
    - `content_hash`: SHA-256 over tenant, request and file hash;
    - `result_hash`: SHA-256 over the row outcomes;
    - `expected_revisions` {account: revision ≥ 1};
    - status `open | committed | expired | superseded`;
    - `created_at`, `expires_at`;
    - the commit result (`committed_at`, `posted_count`, `duplicate_count`). A CHECK makes these present exactly when the status is committed.
  - FORCE RLS on `app.tenant_id` and a tenant FK. Accounts are text ids with no FK, as in 0007.
  - Guard trigger, for any role:
    - inputs are immutable;
    - status only moves from `open`, and never back.
  - **Redaction (review S2):** raw exports can hold personal data (spec §12 retention classes; spec §4 lawful redaction). The trigger lets `file_bytes` become NULL only on a settled preview (committed, expired or superseded), and a CHECK keeps it non-NULL while open. The bytes are never re-filled. `request` (mapping headers, limits) is kept: it holds no row data.
  - No DELETE or TRUNCATE: the 0007 statement trigger refuses them, and no role is granted them.
  - Grants to `qw_app` and `qw_worker`: SELECT; column INSERT of the inputs; column UPDATE of the status and result only. `qw_worker` (the retention job) also has UPDATE on `file_bytes`. `qw_ingest` gets nothing.
  - No SECURITY DEFINER function.
- `import_store.py` (new).
  - **Commit token:** `CommitToken(preview_id, content_hash)`. Its wire form is `"<uuid>:<sha256>"`, since `import_preview.schema.json` only says `commit_token` is a string.
  - **`save_preview`:**
    - It runs the domain `preview` on one journal that holds the stored accounts the file names.
    - Preview time and each observation's `observed_at` are the transaction time. They are stored as `created_at` and reused at commit, so they are never reset.
    - It refuses a request for another tenant and snapshot files.
    - It refuses a file over 8 MiB or `limits.max_bytes` over 8 MiB with `ValueError`, before any database write (review S1).
    - Only a validated preview marks the tenant's open previews that name any of the same accounts as superseded (review N2). Previews saved concurrently do not see each other; the revision check settles them.
    - TTL defaults to 24 h. This is an assumption; the spec sets no TTL.
  - **`commit_preview`, in the caller's transaction:**
    1. `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the preview. An unknown id and another tenant's id give the same answer, `unknown_preview`.
    2. It refuses `token_mismatch`. A committed preview returns its stored result.
    3. It refuses `expired`/`superseded`, and `hash_mismatch` when the stored inputs no longer reproduce the hash. An expired preview is marked so.
    4. It locks the accounts in sorted order and checks `journal_store.revision` against each bound revision; a difference is a `conflict`.
    5. It re-derives the preview from the stored inputs under those locks. A different `result_hash` gives `conflict` `preview_changed`; this is how a SourceMap or SecurityMaster change is caught. A preview that is not validated is rejected.
    6. In a savepoint it calls `observe` for the accepted rows and `post_many` for the events, then marks the preview committed.
  - **`redact_settled_preview(tx, preview_id)`** (runs as `qw_worker`) sets a settled preview's file to NULL and is idempotent. An open preview raises `ValueError`, an unknown id `LookupError`. The hashes and result stay, so a re-commit of a redacted committed preview returns its result, and a redacted expired one stays refused.
  - The commit now checks that every `post_many` outcome is POSTED; otherwise the savepoint rolls back and the result is a `conflict` (review N4).
- `journal_store.py`: `post_many` posts a batch with one replay per account, locking the accounts in sorted order. Each single `post` replays the whole account, so a 10k-row import made of single posts would cost O(n²). It returns every outcome, and its docstring says a non-journal exception leaves the transaction aborted for the caller to roll back.
- **Role:**
  - The import commit writes ledger events, so it runs as `qw_worker` (the 202 job of `commitImport`), or as `qw_app` for an interactive commit. 0007 already grants both.
  - `qw_ingest` writes source records only (T008 §5) and has no access to previews.
- `tests/test_import_store.py` (new, 15 DB tests, SYNTHETIC, as non-superuser `qw_app`/`qw_worker` logins). `test_tenancy.py`: `import_preview` is added to the forced-RLS list.

### Scenarios covered (tests)
- **Round trip:** the stored events equal the in-memory domain `preview` + `commit` of the same file, with the same `CommitResult`. NUM01 cash, quantity, cost and realised P&L match `numerical_oracles.json`. Row counts per table are checked, and `observed_at` equals the preview's `created_at`.
- **Conflicts:** a post to the account between preview and commit gives `conflict` (revision), adds no import rows, and leaves the preview open. A conflicting source record already held for the last row gives `conflict`, and the savepoint rolls back the first two rows' records.
- **Idempotency:** a re-commit returns the same result with no new rows. A re-preview of the same file is all duplicates and commits 0 posted, 3 duplicates.
- **Concurrency:** two concurrent commits of one preview. The second is observed waiting on the row lock, then both return the same result, and there are 3 events.
- **Refusals:** another tenant's id (`unknown_preview`); a forged hash (`token_mismatch`); the same file under another tenant gets another hash; a save for another tenant and malformed token wire forms raise; expired (then marked) and superseded previews, before and after the successor commits; tampered stored bytes in replica mode (`hash_mismatch`); a security master without the symbol (`conflict` `preview_changed`); a needs-resolution preview.
- **Review fixes:**
  - S1: a file of 8 MiB + 1 byte, and `max_bytes` of 8 MiB + 1, raise `ValueError` and store nothing.
  - S2: redacting an open preview is refused by the adapter, the trigger and (in replica mode) the CHECK; `qw_app` lacks the grant. Redacting a committed preview works and is idempotent, a re-commit returns the stored result, and re-filling is refused. A redacted expired preview stays refused.
  - N2: a needs-resolution re-preview leaves the open preview open, and that preview commits.
  - N3: two previews of one account saved in overlapping transactions are both open; the first commit wins and the other gets a revision conflict. A preview of a disjoint account, or in another tenant, supersedes nothing.
- **Guards and catalogue:** a runtime role cannot UPDATE the hash or `created_at`, DELETE or TRUNCATE; a settled status cannot move; the owner's input UPDATE and DELETE are refused by trigger; a committed status without its result fails the CHECK; RLS hides other tenants; the guard is invoker-rights with `search_path=pg_catalog` and there are no definer functions; only `qw_worker` may UPDATE `file_bytes`, no runtime role holds DELETE, TRUNCATE or other input-column UPDATE, and `qw_ingest` cannot SELECT.

### Gaps and limitations (increment 2)
- SourceMap and SecurityMaster are not persisted. The caller supplies them at preview and at commit, and a change is caught only through `result_hash`.
- `CommitImport.expected_account_revision` (one integer) and the `If-Match` mapping (C-04) are route work. The store binds a map of revisions per account.
- Redaction exists but nothing schedules it: a retention job and a retention class for raw import files remain. Rows are never deleted. The owner and a superuser can bypass the triggers, as in 0007.
- A preview loads every named account twice (once to derive, once at commit); both are O(events).
