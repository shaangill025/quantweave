## PR A0 — journal holding ledger (`journal.py`, `test_journal.py`)
- **New on `Journal`:** `journal_id`, `copy()` and `revision(account)` (a per-account counter).
- **Holding ledger:** a per-(account, instrument) ledger of cumulative units and cost in effective-time order.
  - `holding()` and the SELL guard bisect it instead of folding the journal.
  - A back-dated event updates the later sums.
  - `_check` no longer copies the key index.
- **N2, mixed cost currencies:**
  - `holding()`, the SELL guard and `Journal.sell` now raise `JournalError("cost_currency_mixed")` for a holding whose cost was ever posted in two currencies.
  - `materialize` cannot total such a holding either; it raises `CurrencyMismatchError`.
- **Tests (R1):**
  - Derandomized Hypothesis differential property (200 examples, up to 25 operations each) with `materialize` as the oracle. Operations: back-dated buys, sells, raw sells from stale snapshots (one unit over, exactly all, or any), splits, reversals and duplicate replays.
  - After each step, `holding()` must equal the oracle with and without own-record exclusion at three times. The guard must refuse exactly when `materialize` of the events plus the new sale goes negative.
  - Copies are deep and get a new id; revision equals 1 + the number of entries.
  - Also: a mixed-currency test, and a corrected sale whose pending reversal the guard counts.
- **Mutation controls** (each alone, all caught): back-dated update off; own-record exclusion off; guard off; guard off by one; guard too strict; pending reversal ignored; wrong bisection; insertion at the end; mixed-currency check off; shallow ledger copy.
- **Notes for the persistence increment:**
  - N3: `journal_id` and revisions are in-memory and do not survive a restart, and the revision check and the posts are not under one lock. Persistence must bind a stored journal identity and lock the account revisions for the commit.
  - N4: a back-dated insert costs O(later entries of that holding). A preview inserts in effective-time order, so only events already in the journal pay this cost.

## PR A1 — transaction files (`csv_import.py`, `test_csv_import.py`, this receipt)
- **Format `canonical-csv/1`:** the 11 fixture columns plus optional `fee_currency`. When `fee_currency` is unmapped, the fee is assumed to be in the trade currency.
- **File rejection:** bytes, NUL, strict UTF-8 (BOM removed), strict `csv`, rows, columns, header-cell length, duplicate header, missing mapped column, format version. `csv.field_size_limit` is process-global and stays at its default (131072).
- **Mapping:** a versioned `ColumnMapping`. Unmapped headers stay raw in `unknown_fields`, are never screened, and must go through `spreadsheet_safe` on export.
- **Row outcomes:** accepted, duplicate, conflict, or quarantined with one of 18 `Reason` codes.
  - The formula check (`= + - @ TAB CR`) covers mapped text fields only.
  - A quarantined row's record id and unknown fields are cut to 64 characters and neutralized.
  - A zero price is `field_invalid`; a sale of a mixed-currency holding is `mixed_currency`.
  - Rows run in effective-time order on `Journal.copy()`; a preview never mutates the journal.
- **`commit(preview, journal, recorded_at, tenant_id, log)` never raises.**
  - Rejections: `tenant_mismatch` (N1), `journal_mismatch`, an unvalidated preview, or a `JournalError` code such as `recorded_at_order`.
  - A changed account revision is a `conflict`.
  - Events are trial-posted on a copy first.
  - Only accepted rows are observed, so `50.0` versus `50` adds no log conflict.
  - Gap: `SourceMap` and `SecurityMaster` changes after the preview are not bound.
- **Provenance:** import id, SHA-256 file hash, mapping version, and the `SourceRef` on each accepted event and observation.
- **Tests:** 59 SYNTHETIC tests in `test_csv_import.py`.
  - NUM01 from the fixture against `numerical_oracles.json`.
  - Re-import no-op, changed-row conflict, and a duplicate written differently.
  - Binding with distinct reasons; back-dated rows; BOM and CRLF.
  - 29 row-reason cases (1 control); unknown cost; mixed fee and cost currencies; injection; raw unknown fields and retention.
  - 11 file-rejection cases (1 control); format version; mapping.
  - Hypothesis shuffle property (derandomized, 60 examples).
- **Mutation controls** (each alone, all caught): duplicates observed; binding off; commit raising; zero price allowed; unknown fields screened; retention off; plus the 13 controls from round 1.
- **Preview cost** (SYNTHETIC, one account, before tenant/mixed-currency changes):
  - before the ledger, alternating buys and sells grew quadratically: 1,000 rows took 12.04 s;
  - after: 10,000 rows took 1.65 s (buys only) and 1.87 s (alternating);
  - 50,000 rows took 9.42 s and 12.00 s;
  - the defaults are therefore `max_rows` 10,000 and `max_bytes` 2 MiB.
