Review fixes:
- **S1.** The sell cap and the cash cap use the ledger as of the fill stamp (entries effective at or before it). `record` refuses an entry earlier than the ledger's latest effective time (`time_order`), so a simulation against a ledger that already holds later entries raises instead of booking a back-dated fill. Fills within a bar are recorded in stamp order. The reviewer's probe (a sell at 13:20 against units bought at 15:00) is a test: no fill.
- **S2.** No fill is stamped at or after `expires_at` (`expired_in_bar`); an expiry is exclusive for both bar-start and bar-end stamps (round-2 nit N1). A gap fill at a bar start before expiry still fills. Tested with a limit expiring at 13:45.
- **Round-2 N2.** A market order that is capped by cash or units stays live for its unfilled remainder and can fill in a later bar once cash or units are available as of that bar's stamp. That is a partial-fill queue, not a rejection; it ends at `expires_at` or by cancellation.
- **N1.** `simulate` evaluates its orders together from the portfolio as it was before them. Orders already recorded in that portfolio must not be simulated again.

### Rules
The full rules are in the module docstring. Every event returns a new portfolio or a typed `Unavailable`, and every entry goes through `record`. So:
- an identical replay returns the same object;
- a conflicting replay of the same event id raises (for example, a corrected version);
- a back-dated event raises `time_order`;
- nothing touches the real `Journal` or any broker.

- **Cash dividend.** Nothing is credited until the action is known at `as_of`, its pay date is known, and both the pay date and the ex-date are reached (`pay_date_unknown`, `pay_date_not_reached`, `ex_date_not_reached`). Entitlement is the units held strictly before the ex-date's exchange-local midnight, which is the holder of record under T+1. Income is amount × units, stamped at the later of the two local midnights; a due-bill special dividend goes ex after it pays. A currency mismatch is refused. A special dividend on an underlying with open virtual options is refused (`option_adjustment_required`).
- **Split.** Units held before the effective date's local midnight are scaled exactly by the `PositiveRatio`, in `Fraction` arithmetic. Cost and cash are unchanged. The corporate-action model treats a fractional result as `CashInLieu` with an unknown amount, so the split is refused (`cash_in_lieu_terms_unknown`) rather than inventing a price. This also covers a fractional holding before the split. A split on an optioned underlying is refused.
- **Writing options** (`write_option`). A sell to open needs:
  - verified, complete, physically settled terms from `contract_terms` (any block reason is returned as-is);
  - a positive `BID` premium mark for the contract that is fresh at the write time (`check_mark`);
  - a write time before the expiry session close from the calendar.
  
  A covered call needs `units × contracts` free units as of the write. A cash-secured put needs `strike × multiplier × contracts` free cash. The write's own premium does not count toward its own collateral; once the write is booked, that premium is ordinary free cash. The write is refused with `cover_insufficient` otherwise. The fee may not push free cash (cash − all put collateral, including this write's) below zero after the premium is credited (`fee_exceeds_premium`). There is one position per contract. The premium is booked as a liability, as negative cost on the contract instrument.
- **Cover in the fill caps.** Open contracts as of a time are derived from the ledger. Call cover lowers the free units and put collateral lowers the free cash, both as of the fill stamp. Collateral is rounded up. An open contract whose terms have become unusable raises an error instead of counting as zero cover.
- **Expiry** (`settle_expiry`). It settles the open contracts of one contract id. Settlement needs:
  - the expiry session close (21:00Z for the synthetic 2026-11-20) at or before `as_of`;
  - a `LAST` or `REFERENCE` settlement mark, never a bid, ask or mid. It must be observed at or after that close, strictly before the next session open from the calendar, and be fresh at `as_of`;
  - an auto-exercise rule.
  
  The outcome comes from T034's `expiry_expectation`. Worthless releases the cover and realizes the premium. Assignment also delivers or receives the underlying at the strike, with average-cost removal on delivery. An uncertain outcome (in the money below the threshold) or an unavailable one changes nothing. The `Position` moves through `expiry_reached` and then `assignment_reported` or `expired_worthless_reported`, all stamped at the close. A replay with the same settlement mark is a no-op, even at a later `as_of`. A different mark raises an error.

## Review fixes (round 1)
The reviewer (a different model) asked for changes. Each new test below failed against the code before the fix; this was checked by running the tests on the old module. Each fix was then reverted on its own, and every revert failed a test.
- **S1. A fee larger than the premium could leave cash short.**
  - The problem: a put written at a premium of 0.001 on 5000 cash ended with cash 4999.45 against 5000 reserved, and settlement then raised. A covered call could push cash to −0.55.
  - The fix: after the premium and the fee, free cash (cash − all put collateral, including the new collateral) must stay ≥ 0, or the write is refused with `fee_exceeds_premium`.
  - Test `test_fee_above_premium_cannot_leave_cash_short`: the put and call cases above, plus a boundary case where the fee equals the premium (cash stays 5000).
- **S2. A due-bill special dividend was credited before its ex-date.**
  - The problem: a special dividend going ex 10-25 and paying 10-20 was credited on 10-20 from units held at a midnight not yet reached.
  - The fix: it is refused with `ex_date_not_reached` until the ex-date's local midnight. It is stamped at the later of the pay and ex midnights.
  - Test `test_due_bill_special_dividend_waits_for_the_ex_date`: 50 units bought on 10-22 are entitled, giving 150 × 0.25 = 37.5, stamped at 2026-10-25 04:00Z.
- **S3. Settlement accepted any mark kind, observed at any time.**
  - The fix: only `LAST` or `REFERENCE` marks are accepted (`settlement_kind_invalid`). The mark must be observed before the next session open, here 2026-11-23 14:30Z (`settlement_outside_window`).
  - Test `test_settlement_needs_a_closing_kind_inside_the_window`: an ASK mark is refused, a mark observed 5 days late is refused, and a LAST mark settles.
- **N1. An identical replay lost to the later option refusal.** An identical split or special-dividend replay now returns the same portfolio, even after an option was written later. The replay is checked before the `option_adjustment_required` refusal. Test `test_identical_replays_win_over_later_option_refusals`.
- **N2.** A premium mark priced at or below zero is refused with `premium_not_bid` (tested).
- **N3** (premium counts as free cash only after its own write) and **N4** (settles one contract id) are clarified in the docstring and the Rules above. N5 is covered by the tests above.
