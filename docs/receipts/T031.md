### Rules (method `opening_range_breakout/1`; the module docstring has the full list)
- **Range.** The range is `[open, open + 15 min)` of the actual session in the versioned calendar. It is frozen at end + `completion_buffer_seconds` from the configured feed's 1-minute bars that were known by then.
  - Before the freeze, the reason is `range_not_complete`.
  - Each partial case blocks with its own reason:
    - a missing or late minute: `range_interval_missing`;
    - a minute with only a retrospective bar: `range_interval_unconfirmed`;
    - a sequence gap: `range_gap`;
    - the continuity watermark is before the range end: `range_trailing_loss`;
    - a correction known after the freeze: `range_corrected`;
    - high = low: `range_zero`.
- **Feeds and halts.**
  - Any bar from another feed inside the range or the signal window is `feed_switch` (AT066). This includes a harmless concurrent second feed: the rule is one feed per range, and it fails closed.
  - An instrument or market halt that overlaps [open, as_of] is `halted`. Halts are half-open [start, end), so a halt starting exactly at `as_of` blocks and one ending exactly at the open does not.
  - A holiday or a closed day is `no_session`.
- **Breakout (long only, one entry per session).** The breakout bar is the first later bar of the same feed whose close is at least range_high + `breakout_ticks` × tick. The minutes before it must be covered (`signal_interval_missing`). A closed minute after the continuity watermark is `signal_not_confirmed`.
- **Deadlines (from the actual close, including early closes).**
  - exit = close − 10 min;
  - entry = exit − 30 min;
  - review = min(trigger + `review_window_seconds` (at most 60, per AT067), entry).
  - A trigger at or after the entry deadline is `entry_window_closed`. A decision at or after the review deadline is `review_expired`. A later breakout never re-arms the session.
- **Proposal-only checks.** A delayed or EOD latency is `feed_not_realtime`: the range and signal are still returned for research, but there is no candidate. `check_use(FINANCIAL_RECOMMENDATION)` must allow the feed at `as_of`, otherwise `feed_unqualified`.
- **Stop and target.** `adverse_exit_scenario` = range_low (a sizing scenario, not a stop fill) and `risk_per_unit` = breakout close − range_low. `target` is None because the catalogue declares a same-session time exit and no price target.
- **Manifest.** STR-ORB-001 `0.1.0-research`, intraday, stocks and ETFs, `regular_session_only=True`. Its code hash is the module source.

  | Parameter | Default | Range | Source |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `opening_range_minutes` | 15 | 5–60 | catalogue |
  | `signal_bar_minutes` | 1 | 1–1 | catalogue |
  | `completion_buffer_seconds` | 30 | 5–300 | research choice |
  | `breakout_ticks` | 1 | 1–100 | research choice |
  | `exit_minutes_before_close` | 10 | 1–60 | research choice |
  | `min_action_window_minutes` | 30 | 5–120 | research choice |
  | `review_window_seconds` | 60 | 1–60 | AT067 cap |

  The config must hash to the adopted `config_hash`, otherwise the reason is `config_mismatch` or `config_invalid`. The catalogue's `outside_regular_session: false` is bound through the `regular_session_only` manifest field rather than as a parameter.

## Negative/adverse cases
- **Hand oracle (2026-10-08 EDT).** The range is [13:30Z, 13:45Z), frozen at 13:45:30Z, with high 101.20 and low 99.40. The threshold is 101.21. The 13:46 bar closes at 101.50 and is known at 13:47:02Z. Risk per unit is 2.10. The review deadline is 13:48:02Z, entry 19:20Z and exit 19:50Z. A close equal to the high is not a breakout, and `breakout_ticks=50` suppresses the signal.
- **Partial ranges.**
  - A missing minute is blocked.
  - A minute known after the buffer is blocked.
  - A gap is blocked.
  - A retrospective-only minute is blocked.
  - A watermark at 13:44:30 or none is blocked.
  - A decision at 13:45:29 is blocked and one at 13:45:30 is allowed.
  - A correction known before the freeze is used (high 101.30). One known after the freeze blocks.
  - A zero range is blocked.
- **Provider switch.** Each of these blocks:
  - a second feed's bar inside the range;
  - a feed change at 13:40;
  - a breakout bar on another feed.
- **Halts.**
  - These block: an instrument halt during the range, a market halt before entry, and an open-ended halt.
  - A halt starting exactly at `as_of` blocks (this was a review fix; the test failed before the change from `<` to `<=`).
  - These do not block: another instrument's halt, a halt that ended exactly at the open, and a halt starting after `as_of`.
- **Sessions.** On the 2026-11-27 early close the deadlines are exit 17:50Z and entry 17:20Z. The 2026-11-26 holiday gives `no_session`. A breakout at 19:20 gives `entry_window_closed`.
- **Review.** A decision at exactly the deadline is `review_expired`, and a later breakout bar does not re-arm the session. `check_review` passes at 13:48:01 and fails at 13:48:02. A 15 s window gives a 13:47:17 deadline, and 61 s is `config_invalid`.
- **DST.** Deadlines follow the local session. On 2026-03-06 (EST) and 2026-11-02 (EST) they are exit 20:50Z and entry 20:20Z. On 2026-03-09 (EDT) they are exit 19:50Z and entry 19:20Z.
- **Watermark.** A watermark at 13:46:30, after the range end and before the breakout minute ends, gives `signal_not_confirmed`. The range is kept and there is no signal (a review fix; the test failed first).
- **Feeds.** A delayed feed is research-only: the range and signal are present, with no candidate. A feed not qualified for financial recommendation is blocked.
- **Gate (T027).** With an intraday policy the manifest is actionable in the gate test world. With `regular_session_only=False` the gate refuses it with exactly `session_not_permitted`.
- **Property (150 examples).** The test toggles a missing or retrospective minute, a gap, a second feed, delayed latency, a halt, trailing loss, an unqualified feed, a holiday, and the decision offset. Its oracle is computed from the toggles alone: a candidate exists exactly when no toggle is set and the offset is in [0 s, 60 s).
- **Mutation controls.** Each of three mutants was applied in place, run, and then restored from a scratch copy; the clean run was confirmed (26 passed). Each was killed:
  - halt check disabled: 1 test failed;
  - review expiry loosened by one window: 3 failed;
  - latency check removed: 1 failed.

## Unresolved gates
- **Coverage accounting.** `coverage.py` (PR #60) is on the base but not used. Switching is not a small change: `coverage()` takes subscription windows rather than a watermark, and it reads bars of all feeds. Until then, a quiet minute with no eligible trades has no bar and blocks (fails closed).
- **Caller-supplied facts.**
  - Gaps, the continuity watermark and halts are caller-supplied facts known at `as_of`, because calendars record no halt knowledge time.
  - `as_of` must be the server clock.
  - The tick size is caller-supplied.
- **Not yet modelled.** No `FeedLatency` class for indicative feeds. No volume, spread or slippage criterion.
- **Not yet bound.** `OrbCandidate` is not bound into `proposals.ProposalVersion`, and the risk engine does not size it yet.
- **Evidence.** All data is SYNTHETIC, and the evidence axis stays `none`. The defaults are unqualified research choices (R072).
- **Not exercised beyond the gate.** R008, R009, R083, R084 and R099 are not exercised by this increment beyond the existing gate and bar modules.
