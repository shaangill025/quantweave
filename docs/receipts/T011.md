## Increment 2, PR B — corporate actions and option adjustment

Branch `t011-corporate-actions-v2`, base `acad0b5` (PR A, #8, which holds the identity, calendar and instants hardening and its tests). Pure domain code, stdlib only (`fractions`, `re`). No dependencies, DB, provider or network; no ledger postings (T012).

### Changes
- `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/corporate_actions.py` (new):
  - `PositiveRatio` is exact, in lowest terms and bounded by 10**18. It is built from two ints, or with `from_decimal` from a decimal string or `Decimal`; floats are rejected.
  - `SourceRef`, plus an event header with `event_id`, `version`, `instrument_id`, `effective: date`, `source` and `known_at` (aware UTC).
  - Events: `Split`/reverse split (1:1 rejected), `StockDividend`, `SymbolChange`, `CashDividend` (amount `Money` > 0; ex = `effective`; optional record, pay and announcement dates; `special`), and `Merger`/`SpinOff` with `terms_known`/`terms_unknown` status.
  - `CorporateActionLog` is append-only. A correction is version n+1 of the same id, kind and instrument, known strictly later. It may change the effective date and terms. It may not change the instrument: effects already derived for the first instrument would lose their cause, so a wrong instrument is corrected by a new event id. Point-in-time reads use `current` and `events(known_as_of)`.
  - `apply_to_master` returns a new `SecurityMaster`. A symbol change reuses `rename` after checking the old ticker; nothing else changes identity.
  - `adjust_option` returns `terms_version + 1`, adjusted and unverified, and appends (event_id, version) to `applied_actions`. Re-applying an absorbed event id is rejected. Deliverables are computed per original contract: units × exact ratio; whole units stay deliverable; a fractional remainder becomes `CashInLieu` with an unknown amount; special-dividend and merger cash add `CashDeliverable`; unknown terms add `UnknownDeliverable`. Strike, multiplier and `underlying_id` are unchanged. How a post-merger underlying is represented is T034 work. Verified terms replace the computed ones (F-14).
  - `replay_option(base, log, known_as_of, effective_as_of)` rebuilds terms from base terms and is how corrections are absorbed.
    - Caller contract: only actions effective during the contract's life apply. Events effective after `effective_as_of` or after the expiry session date are skipped.
    - Order: effective date, then same-day precedence by kind (splits, stock dividends, cash, spin-offs, mergers, symbol changes), then event id. Flooring makes a split and a stock dividend non-commutative, so the precedence is a documented convention that verified terms override.
- `options.py`:
  - New components: `CashInLieu` and `UnknownDeliverable`, with block reasons `cash_in_lieu_unknown` and `deliverable_terms_unknown`; a contract carrying either cannot be marked verified.
  - New field: `applied_actions`, with each event id at most once.
  - Increment 1 follow-ups: `terms_version` must be an int; deliverables must be a tuple with no duplicate component; `underlying_id != contract_id`; OCC expiry must be a `date`.

### Recorded limitation and assumption
- Ordinary cash dividends never adjust option terms, and `special` is supplied by the source. A mislabelled extraordinary dividend leaves a verified contract unchanged. Quarantine of unknown or uncertain actions is T012.
- Spec 04 states no dividend date order. Enforced where supplied: announced ≤ ex and ≤ record, record ≤ pay, and for ordinary dividends ex ≤ record and ex ≤ pay. `special` skips the ex-date order (due-bill case). Owner to confirm.

### Tests and checks (SYNTHETIC, hand-computed)
- `test_corporate_actions.py` (24 tests) and additions to `test_options.py`.
  - Split and other adjustments: 100×2:1 = 200; 75×3:2 = 112 + ½ in lieu; 100×1:3 = 33 + ⅓; 5% stock dividend 100 → 105; merger at 0.5 + USD 10 → 50 shares + USD 1000; spin-off 1:3 → 100 + 33 + ⅓.
