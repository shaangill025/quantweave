## Changes
- `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/identity.py`: `InstrumentId` (UUID, separate type from `Ticker`), `Mic` (ISO 10383), `Ticker`, `ProviderId` (namespace + external id), `Listing` and `ProviderLink` with half-open `[valid_from, valid_to)` validity, `SecurityMaster` (add, all-or-nothing `rename`, `resolve(ticker, mic | None, as_of)`, `ticker_as_of`, `resolve_provider`). `Resolution` is `found`, `ambiguous` (with candidates) or `unknown`; it never guesses. Rejected: the same (MIC, ticker) on two instruments over overlapping dates, one instrument with two tickers on one MIC at once, and a provider id mapped twice over overlapping dates.
- `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/options.py`: `OptionContract` (contract and underlying ids, `expiry: SessionRef` with calendar id, strike `Price` + currency, right, style, settlement, `multiplier: Multiplier | None`, deliverables of `UnitDeliverable(PositiveQuantity)` / `CashDeliverable(Money)`, `adjusted`, `terms_verified`, `terms_version`). `sizing_block_reasons()` returns `terms_unverified`, `multiplier_unknown`, `deliverables_unknown`, `exercise_style_unknown`, `settlement_unknown`. Verified terms must be complete. There is no default multiplier. `OccSymbol` parses and formats the 21-character OCC form (root padded to 6, YYMMDD read as 20YY, strike in thousandths) and carries no multiplier or deliverable.
- `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/calendars.py`: `CalendarId`, `SessionRef`, `CalendarVersion` (date range, regular open/close, weekend, holidays, early closes, ad-hoc closures), `ExchangeCalendar` (non-overlapping versions, one IANA zone via `zoneinfo`), `Session` (carries calendar id and version), `Halt` (market-wide or per instrument, open-ended allowed), `MarketState`. Functions: `session_for`, `is_trading_day`, `state_at`/`is_open`, `next_open`/`next_close` (strictly after; halts ignored), `add_trading_days`, `trading_days_between`, `load_calendar` (rejects JSON numbers). Boundary semantics: sessions and halts are half-open, so the market is open at the opening instant and closed at the closing instant. Dates outside every version raise `CalendarCoverageError`.
- `packages/domain/tests/fixtures/xnys_synthetic.json`: **SYNTHETIC/TEST** XNYS data, two versions, a few 2026–2027 holidays, two 13:00 early closes and one invented ad-hoc closure (2026-12-31). It does not claim real holiday completeness. The authoritative calendar source and its licence are a T021/T022 qualification item (T008 A-03).
- Tests: `test_identity.py`, `test_options.py`, `test_calendars.py`. Expected values are hand-computed (New York UTC-5/UTC-4 offsets). Property tests (hypothesis, derandomized) cover: accepted listings never overlap, checked by an independent day scan; the OCC round trip; trading-day arithmetic agreeing with `session_for` through an independent counting oracle.
- `check_no_float` MONEY_PATHS and the boundary allowlist are unchanged: no package was added, and `packages/domain/src` is already scanned.

## Negative/adverse cases
- Rejected: malformed MIC, ticker, UUID or provider id (including full-width characters); empty intervals; overlapping listings and provider links; a rename onto a taken ticker, which leaves the registry unchanged; an OCC symbol with an unpadded root, bad right, impossible date, zero strike, trailing newline, embedded space or non-ASCII digit; an unrepresentable strike or year; verified-but-incomplete terms; a contract that delivers itself; naive datetimes; overlapping calendar versions, close before open, early close outside hours, an unknown zone, numeric weekend values, an invalid date, a special date outside its version; out-of-coverage queries.
- Mutation controls, applied one at a time and reverted: inclusive close, ignoring `terms_verified`, removing the overlap check, ignoring ad-hoc closures, right-padding the OCC root and ignoring the calendar zone. Each made 2 to 8 tests fail.
