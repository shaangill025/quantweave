## Negative/adverse cases (SYNTHETIC)
- **Deletion.** The journal holds a deposit and a buy of 5 units. After adding, removing and re-adding an entry and then deleting the list, the journal's `entries()`, account revision and `materialize` result (5 units) are identical. An AST check over `import` and `from` nodes, followed transitively through every `qw_domain` module `watchlists.py` reaches, finds neither `journal` nor `postings`. As a positive control, the same walk does find `journal` from `reconciliation`.
- **CRUD.** Stale revisions, both behind and ahead, are refused for rename and delete: nothing changes and no audit record is written. Refusals: `entry_exists`, `entry_missing`, `archived`, `id_used`. Malformed priority, naive dates, list tags, a non-InstrumentId and a malformed actor are rejected.
- **Tenancy.** The same id in two tenants is kept apart. A third tenant gets `not_found` on get, rename and delete. Audit and demands are per tenant. `AlertBook` refuses another tenant's rule.
- **Hand values.**
  - Cross over 100: 99.50→100.25 and 100→100.01 fire; 100.25→101 and 99→100 do not. 100.50→99.99 under 100 fires.
  - Percent move: 50→51 (+2%) fires for gte 2 but not gt 2. 80→77.6 (−3%) fires for lte −3; 80→77.61 (−2.9875%) does not. Between 1 and 2: 200→203 and 200→202 (inclusive) fire; 200→205 does not.
  - Volume: 1500 against gt 1000 fires; against gt 1500 it does not. A covered interval with no bar is `feed_unverifiable`. Review probe: a `volume lte 0` rule bound to feed-other, given only the IEX feed's silence, does not fire (`feed_unverifiable`).
  - Review: not due at 13:59, fires at 14:00.
- **Stale inputs (never fire).** Missing coverage (sequence gap), delayed (not streamed), retrospective, older than 2 minutes, a single interval, non-contiguous intervals, a covered interval without a bar (`feed_unverifiable`), an unconfigured threshold, another feed. A look-ahead (open interval, or a bar known after t) is refused.
- **Lifecycle.** Same trigger gives a duplicate. A new trigger 9 min 59 s after the last alert, with a 10-minute cooldown, is suppressed by the cooldown and stays consumed. Re-snoozing an active snooze without `extend` is refused. A trigger during a snooze is recorded as snoozed. A stale evaluation is recorded and does not consume its trigger. Out-of-order offers are refused.
- **Property.** Random advances, outcomes, triggers, cooldowns and snooze requests. No alert fires on a stale or untriggered input, during cooldown (checked against the log), during snooze (tracked by the test), or for a trigger already offered. A fresh, due trigger always alerts.
- **Mutation controls.** Worker self-report, run in a scratchpad copy, each mutant reverted. 29 mutants (re-run after the review fix, including dropping the `feed_unverifiable` check), 28 killed; three tests had been strengthened earlier (inclusive `between`, a bar known after t, merge order). The one survivor is equivalent: it adds an unused seen key on a stale offer.

## Negative/adverse cases (SYNTHETIC)
- **Versioning.** Stale `expected` values (behind, ahead, 0) are refused and write nothing. The API covers a missing If-Match (428) and `"2"`, `W/"1"`, `1`, `"01"`, `*` and lists (412).
- **Idempotency.** Each case is checked at both the store and the API: a same-key replay of the same request, a different request reusing the key (409), another principal's identical key (a new list), an update replay after a later revision, and a delete replay after the tombstone.
- **Tenancy.** Tenant B gets 404 on read, update and delete, and an empty list. A cursor is refused when it comes from another principal, belongs to another route (`/policies`), or has been forged. Uppercase, unhyphenated and braced UUID spellings are refused.
- **Database guards (as qw_app).** Refused: a skipped revision, a wrong previous hash, another user's session, the user's own revoked or expired session, `create` at revision 3, a create 120 s back, a next revision an hour ahead, a revision 30 s back that is inside the window but before the previous one, and any row after a tombstone. As qw_app, tenant B counts 0 of tenant A's rows (A counts 2). Two clean rows are accepted as positive controls. UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE are refused, also for the superuser through the append-only trigger.
- **Idempotency.** After the idempotency record is purged, create, update and delete replays are answered from the store with the same body, and the audit counts stay at 1 each. The same key with another update body, or a delete with another If-Match, is 409. Another principal using the same key on update writes a new revision; it is not a replay.
- **Paging boundary.** Two lists at limit 2 give `next_cursor` null.
- **Tampering (superuser, triggers off).** Eleven edits, including a 1 s forward time move that only the hash catches, fail closed as `integrity` on load, page, update and delete; restoring the rows restores reads. Re-hashed rows (so the hash check passes) still fail: non-empty entries, a tombstone that renames the list, a revision gap (2 to 3), a wrong previous hash, and a time before the previous revision. The previous-hash and time cases are caught only by the chain check; the revision gap is also caught by the domain replay. API: list, read and update give 409 and never echo the forged name.
- **AT057.** The API test seeds the reads-test journal (10 units bought, hand-computed 9492.55 CAD ledger cash). After a full create, update and delete it reads `readSnapshot` and gets an identical result. The store imports nothing from the journal, postings, imports or proposals, and the table's foreign keys are exactly membership and session.
- **Mutation controls.** Each mutant was applied in this worktree, the targeted tests were run, and the file was restored.
  - Serial run: 24 mutants, 21 killed. Killed: entries rule, tombstone snapshot, replay revision and content checks (update/delete and create), stale check, deleted-is-404, page hiding deleted lists, strict page `after`, description and time in the hash, the SQL session, after-tombstone, revision-step and append-only guards, forced RLS, 428, weak-ETag acceptance, audit only on write, integrity mapped to 500, and serving the description.
  - The first serial run left `sql-clock-window` and `hash-omits-time` alive and showed the append-only and audit gates untested. The tests were strengthened (isolated clock cases with controls, a hash-only tamper, superuser append-only, a purged-record replay). Re-run: all of these killed. For the clock mutant the failure was confirmed to be "DID NOT RAISE" on the isolated case.
  - Two survivors are equivalent. `canonical-id`: the chain lookup is keyed by the exact id string, so another spelling still misses. `nothing-after-delete` at load: replaying a rename or delete after a tombstone already fails in the domain.
  - Review follow-up run: 14 mutants, one at a time. After each, every source file's SHA-256 was compared with its pre-mutation backup; all matched. 13 killed: the chain's previous-hash and time checks, the SQL revoked, expired and before-previous guards, an RLS policy of `USING (true)`, ignoring the principal in key replays, `if wrote:` replaced with `if True:` for create, update and delete, update and delete idempotency payloads without the body or the If-Match, and `>=` at the page boundary. The survivor `chain-rev` (dropping `rev == len(chain) + 1`) is equivalent except for one case. The domain replay refuses any revision gap in an update or delete as `version_conflict`, which the re-hashed revision case confirms. The exception is a lone `create` row stored at revision 2 or higher with a recomputed hash. The domain would accept it as a mis-numbered first row. Reaching it needs a superuser to bypass the table CHECKs `(revision = 1) = (action = 'create')` and `(revision = 1) = (previous_hash IS NULL)`. The adapter check is kept for that reason.
  - Process note: an earlier pair of mutation runs overlapped by mistake and left three mutants in the tree. These were found by checking every target, restored, and confirmed by reading the files in full. All results above come from later runs with nothing running alongside.
