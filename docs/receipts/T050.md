## Changes
- New `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/optimizer_improvement.py`; new `packages/domain/tests/test_optimizer_improvement.py` (7 test functions, SYNTHETIC data, hand-computed expectations); this receipt.
- **Artifact.** `OptimizerConfig` is a closed policy over the T046 extraction knobs: budget 1–8, ordered operators, selection `first_improving` or `best_of_budget`. Other keys are refused: `widens_authority` for tests, thresholds, risk, verification, gates, release, signing, assessor, approval, caps, limits, depth or budget, else `not_adaptable`. `content_hash` covers canonical behaviour (round 1, N3): it drops operators past the budget, budget past the reachable moves, the rule when there is one proposal, and operator order in a full best-of search (ties keep the lower hash). Such relabels are `identical_relabel` at propose time. Depth, patience and caps are owner `Limits`, outside the artifact.
- **Cycle and runner gate.** `run_cycle` is a fixed host interpreter on a `development` fixture. Its receipt records the runner, executed policy, authority, input hashes, scored proposals, selection and declared cost. `runner_authority` lets only the protocol baseline run while nothing is installed. After that, only the installed head runs, and only when all of these hold: it is runnable now and was installed before the run (`not_yet_installed`); it is this experiment's single candidate for the hash, proposed from the baseline's run (`not_proposed`, `ambiguous_candidate`, `not_from_baseline`; B1); its latest verdict accepts, cites trials and covers every scorecard metric (`assessment_skipped`, `not_accepted:*`, `verdict_without_evidence`; B2), its scores pass every scorecard threshold (`verdict_inconsistent`; round 2, N-A) and carries no synthetic or gate-lifted limitation unless the test-only `accept_synthetic_evidence=True` is passed (`synthetic_evidence`; strict on every path, including no install, because the internal flag is a required keyword with no default, round 2, D1); the release names that verdict (`release_binding`), with approval and install at or after it (`before_verdict`); neither the producer nor the assessor approved it (`self_approval`) and the producer did not install it (`self_install`); and the assessor shares no actor, route or family with the producer, case-insensitively, and carries no optimizer version (`self_certification`; N1). V0 becomes `superseded` once V1 is installed.
- **Self-change.** A `SelfProposer` port; none gives `Blocked(generator_not_configured)`, and the only proposer is a SYNTHETIC test double. The candidate's parent is the baseline; it records the runner hash, version, source cycle and source output hash. Bounds: `depth_limit`; a monthly cap of at most USD20 (`cap_exceeds_owner_budget`); a positive cost per proposal; non-negative declared costs on receipts and in history (`negative_cost`, B3), summed per UTC calendar month whatever the record's offset (round 2, N-B); `stop_no_progress`. `assess_self_change` refuses optimizer identities, then runs T044 `assess`.
- **Recursion checks.** `verify_recursion` returns a `Verification` that is always `provisional` and lists `receipts_unauthenticated` (N2): receipt ids, times, authority and costs are self-asserted, so it is necessary, not sufficient. It re-executes both cycles and checks: `receipt_not_reproduced`, `runner_config_mismatch`, `v0_not_baseline`, `identical_relabel`, `replayed_output`, `source_cycle` (id and output hash), the gate's assessment checks, runnability at the V1 run, `ran_before_install`, `runner_authority` and `next_lineage` (V1 hash, version and cycle, and the selected artifact).

`ReceiptProposer` is a real `SelfProposer`. It reads only the receipt's executed policy, proposals and selection; it ignores ids, times, costs and fixtures. Rules:
- **R1:** append missing operators, in enum order.
- **R2:** put the selected proposal's operator first; if nothing improved, put the tried operators last.
- **R3:** raise a spent budget to every move the operators can make (at most 8).
- **R4:** switch `first_improving` to `best_of_budget` when the cycle stopped with moves and budget left.

Refusals: a result with V0's behaviour raises `no_change`. A receipt that is not of the current policy, or names a pick it never proposed, raises `receipt_mismatch`. The output is the three knobs only and still passes through `from_payload`, `identical_relabel` and the recording of the source cycle and its output hash.

Recursion: from V0's receipt it derives V1 = budget 3, (date, value), first_improving. From V1's receipt it derives V2 = `best_of_budget`, and V2 is a fixed point (`no_change`). An exhaustive test over 192 policy and base receipts finds no relabel and no output beyond the three knobs.

`measure` is the assessor's side. Each policy runs its cycle (the search) from the source cycle's research base on the development fixture that the source cycle ran on. The config each search selects is then scored on the protected, plan-bound promotion fixture. `promotion_gain` is therefore an out-of-sample difference. The two slots cannot be swapped: the development slot refuses a non-development fixture (`not_development`) and one the source cycle did not run on (`source_fixture`); the scoring slot refuses a non-promotion fixture (`not_promotion`).

Other refusals:
- `self_certification` (optimizer identities, including V1's version) and `assessor_not_registered`;
- `test_access_changed`, `answer_key_changed` and `evidence_class`;
- `baseline_hash`, `not_from_baseline` (the candidate's parent is not V0's version), `source_cycle`, `base_mismatch` and `candidate_experiment`;
- `cost_exceeds_limits`.

Metrics: `promotion_gain`, `replayed_output`, `payload_invalid`, `identical_relabels`, `threshold_changes` and `cost_usd` (Decimal; V1's declared worst-case cycle cost). A payload that is unparsable, does not match the artifact hash or has stray keys is measured as V0, so it fails closed.

`scorecard(id, max_cost, limits)`. Its goals are `promotion_gain` ≥ 1, which replaces the `dev_gain` the producer could see, and `cost_usd` ≤ `max_cost`, the same pattern as the research scorecard. `max_cost` must be greater than 0 and at most the owner's `Limits.monthly_cap`. `check_protocol` (run by `measure` and the runner gate) requires:
- exactly one non-hard `promotion_gain` AT_LEAST goal with a bound of at least 1 (`gain_goal_missing`), so a cost-only scorecard or a gain bound of −5 cannot let the budget-only loser through;
- no `dev_gain` threshold at all (`dev_gain_goal`);
- a single positive cost goal within USD20 (`cost_bound_missing`).

`measure` also refuses a protocol whose cost goal is above the measuring owner's limits.

A new test shows a budget-only V1 (budget 3 instead of 1) that wins on development data, where first_date reads `DEV2`'s Filed lines (2 + 2 = 4 > 2). On the promotion fixture it scores 4 against V0's 6, so `promotion_gain` = −2 and the goal fails. Searching on the promotion fixture itself would have selected FIX and shown a gain, which is why the search no longer runs there. A same-behaviour payload that declares budget 8 costs USD4.00 > USD2.00 and fails only the cost goal.
