### Rules
- **One book per scope and UTC calendar month.** The spec names no budget timezone, so UTC applies (spec §3). A reservation timed in another month is refused (`wrong_period`).
- **Caps.** Every category draws on the inference cap. Improvement also has its own cap inside it, so USD20 of improvement counts toward the USD100 (§11). Committed = open reservations + spent, where spent is the actual for settled reservations and the full reservation for expired ones. F-18: committed ≤ cap, for the whole book and for improvement.
- **Retries.** A retry with the same id and the same terms returns the book unchanged (no new `created_at` or deadline), but only while the reservation is still reserved and not dispatched. A released, expired, settled or dispatched reservation is refused (`reservation_closed`). The same id with different terms is refused (`duplicate_reservation`).
- **Dispatch.** `dispatched(id, at)` is refused at or after the reservation deadline (`deadline_passed`) and once the owner authorization ref is no longer set (`spending_not_authorized`).
- **Settling.** `settle` needs a dispatched reservation (or an expired one) and a report time no earlier than the reservation (`report_before_reservation`). It records the reported actual, which releases the remainder. The charge always stays in the reservation's month, even when the report arrives in a later month (tested across 2026-10/2026-11). An actual above the reservation is recorded as reported, not truncated. It makes `available` negative and blocks new reservations.
- **Releasing and expiry.** `release` works only on a reservation that was never dispatched. A dispatched reservation is never released: it stays reserved until a usage report arrives or its deadline passes. `expire_due` then charges it at the full reservation (status `expired`), and a later report can still settle it. Undispatched reservations past their deadline are released.
- **Atomicity.** The book is immutable and carries a `version`. `apply(current, new, expected_version)` installs a new book only if `current` is still at `expected_version`; otherwise it raises `stale_version`. Persistence must do the same in one transaction (`UPDATE ... WHERE version = expected`). The in-memory test shows the protocol, not database atomicity: 20 threads wait on a `threading.Barrier` so all compute from the same book, write through `apply` under a lock, and retry on `stale_version`. The test asserts at least one compare-and-set failure. A control that writes without the version check loses updates (more grants than reservations in the final book).
- **Late reports and authentication.** A late usage report overwrites the worst-case charge of an expired reservation. The gateway must therefore authenticate the reporter (only the provider adapter for the admitted route) before calling `settle`.
- **Rounding.** The book takes amounts as given; rounding cost up to the micro-dollar is the metering code's job (gateway increment).

## Negative cases (SYNTHETIC)
- **Caps.** A reservation one micro-dollar over the inference cap is refused, and the book is unchanged. The improvement cap is enforced, and improvement exhaustion still leaves monitoring available (AT032). Improvement is also blocked by the inference cap.
- **Configuration.** Spending is refused without an owner authorization. Bad config is rejected: a JSON number, improvement above inference, a negative cap, or a missing key.
- **Settling and expiry.**
  - Settlement is final.
  - An overrun is recorded and blocks further reservations.
  - A dispatched reservation cannot be released.
  - Nothing expires one microsecond before the deadline. At the deadline the call is charged at the worst case, and a late report corrects the charge.
  - An undispatched reservation cannot be settled.
- **Inputs.** The UTC month boundary is applied to a −05:00 local time. Naive datetimes, float money, Decimal passed in place of `UsdBudget`, and zero or negative amounts are all rejected.
- **Concurrency.** 20 barrier-synchronised threads race for USD7 each against USD100 through `apply`. At least one compare-and-set conflict occurs; exactly 14 are granted, for USD98 in total, and the final book holds all 14. Without the version check (control) updates are lost. `apply` refuses stale versions.
- **Closed reservations.** Reviewer probes: reserve → release → re-reserve, and dispatch → expire → re-reserve, are both refused (`reservation_closed`); so are dispatched and settled ids.
- **Dispatch guards.** Dispatch at the deadline or after the authorization is removed is refused; one microsecond before the deadline it succeeds.
- **Property.** Under random reserve, dispatch, settle, release and expire sequences, committed never exceeds either cap and no actual exceeds its reservation. This holds only because the property reports actuals at or below the reservation; a real overrun is recorded as reported and can take committed above a cap (tested separately).
- **Oracles.** Expected amounts are hand-computed.
