Design decisions: (1) As of 2026-10-16, the view called `GET /experiments` although it was not served yet (served since #126), following the T054 option-terms precedent; it works unchanged once the API serves the contract route. (2) No scorecard or verdict shape was invented (unlike the T054 scorecard), because the contract has none. (3) "Not proven" is unconditional in this increment: even with every gate open, the client cannot verify a signed receipt, installed hash or runner receipt, so it never claims proof. (4) No `/session` read: with no governance operation served, no role can act, so nothing is role-dependent yet. (5) Review round 1 asked how to treat the four targets without an improvement feature. I chose to show a related feature and fail closed, not to map it as the target's gate. `qw_domain.capabilities` serves `ai_research`, `ai_evaluator` and `strategy_proposals`, but these gate user-facing capabilities, not the improvement paths. For example, `ai_research` lists model-route and evidence gates, but none for sandbox, independent assessment, human promotion or experiment budget. So an open related feature does not show that the improvement path is qualified. The row says "No served gate specific to improvement for this target" and names the related feature and its state. A closed or unknown related feature still blocks reliance on states past the gate, and an open one leaves them "unverified". (6) No money or timestamps are in the experiment schema; request times are shown in UTC through the shared `Retrieved`.
