### Rules (method `momentum_trend_next_session/1`)
The module docstring states the full rules. In brief: finalized regular-session daily closes visible at t (latest version; retrospective bars only from their recovery time); splits known at t applied, with `split_adjustment_mismatch` when the feed's declared adjustment basis disagrees; a split known at t effective after the signal session and on or before the entry session blocks both the signal and sizing (`split_before_entry:<id>`), because the spec says adjustment discontinuities prevent use (no unit conversion); momentum C_s/C_(s-126) - 1 > 0 and C_s above the 200-close mean; earliest action at the next regular-session open after t, with the reference fill at that open, never the signal close; deterministic exits (invalidation, trend break, split-adjusted stop, time); volatility-stop sizing (mean absolute close change x k) capped by cash and the policy planned-loss budget, floored to the lot, as input to `risk.evaluate`. Precondition: the estimate mark is quoted in the same currency as the bars. Bars carry no currency, so this is not checked. Manifest parameters: `momentum_sessions` 126 (2-504), `trend_sessions` 200 (2-756), `volatility_sessions` 20 (2-252), `stop_volatility_multiple` 2 (0.5-10), `max_holding_sessions` 126 (1-756). The config must hash to the adopted one, and the T027 `actionable` gate refuses the manifest.

## Interpretations (owner review)
- The session counts come from the catalogue. The volatility window, stop multiple and holding limit are unqualified research choices, because the spec gives no values. The planned-loss budget is the caller's adopted-policy amount; no percentage is embedded.
- The prices are a price-return series: cash dividends are not adjusted. Unexplained discontinuities are not detected automatically.

## Negative/adverse cases
- **Hand oracles.** For closes 10, 11, 12, 11, 13 (m=3, w=5, v=2): momentum 2/11, mean 57/5 and volatility 3/2. For closes 100..299 at the default 126/200: momentum 126/173 and mean 199.5. The 18-digit roundings were checked independently with Python `Decimal`.
- **No look-ahead.** The signal does not change when the next session's bar, a correction known after t, a retrospective bar recovered after t, or a split announced after t is added. A bar for the signal session that is known only after t gives `missing_session`, with no stale substitute. The Hypothesis property is differential: it compares the full input against the input pre-filtered to what was known at t. It shows only that no input unknown at t is used; value correctness rests on the hand-computed tests.
- **Splits.** Raw 20, 22, 24, 11, 13 with a 2:1 split known at t give the same signal as the adjusted series. With the split announced after t, the raw values are used (not eligible). A provider series already adjusted for the split is not adjusted twice. A mismatch is refused when the basis applies a split unknown at t, or when it is version 1 while the known split was corrected to version 2. A split known at t that is effective on 3 July (a holiday between signal and entry) or on the entry session gives `split_before_entry` in `evaluate`. `size_entry` gives the same result for a signal computed before the split was known. A split effective after the entry session does not block.
- **Other cases.** Insufficient history 4/5 and 199/200, a hole, a conflicting retrospective bar, an unknown basis, a stock dividend in the window, a holiday (the next open skips 3 July), every exit reason including split-adjusted stops, the sizing block reasons, a config mismatch or out-of-bounds config, and the gate refusal.
- **Mutation controls.** Five mutants were applied and then restored, and each was killed: reading bars 9 days ahead (3 failures), ignoring split known times (4 failures), allowing fills 4 days early (1 failure), excluding the entry session from the split check (1 failure), and removing the sizing split check (1 failure).
