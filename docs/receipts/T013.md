  - `UnderlyingAccount` (tenant, institution, account type, base currency) is separate from `Source` (`SourceKind`: broker API, broker export, aggregator, manual, mirror).
  - `SourceMap.remap` appends a `MappingRevision` for one (source, account reference): half-open intervals, each naming exactly one account of the source's tenant, no overlaps, knowledge time non-decreasing, validated before anything changes. `resolve(..., known_as_of)` replays any earlier revision; history is never rewritten.
  - `consolidate`: snapshots after `as_of` are ignored; an unknown source or a reference with no mapping in force is quarantined (`unknown_source`, `unmapped`); a snapshot from another tenant's source is refused (`foreign_tenant`), so it never enters a result. Per account, each source's latest snapshot counts; snapshots older than `max_age` do not vote (stale-only accounts are `stale`).
  - Authority policy `source_authority/1` (`AUTHORITY`): broker API > broker export > aggregator > manual > mirror, then newer, then source id. One figure per (account, item) from the top candidate. A `complete` snapshot reports omitted items as zero; an incomplete one (mirror) says nothing about them. Any fresh disagreement is an `ItemConflict` listing every candidate: the account is `conflicted` and sizing-blocked, the authoritative figure is shown, nothing is averaged or summed, and other accounts continue (spec §4).
  - Cash is per account and currency; its basis is `broker_available` only from broker or aggregator sources, otherwise `user_reported` and never spendable (spec §4 "Do not claim real buying power").
  - `PortfolioScope` (selected accounts, all disclosed, hypothetical only; outside context none, summarized-unverified or unknown) with `Sleeve`s per account and currency. `apply_scope` gives exposure per (instrument, currency) with no FX; `spendable_cash(account, currency)` reads only that account and currency; `sleeve_available` returns only the sleeve's own allocation and nothing when the account/currency is over-allocated or its cash unknown. `analysis_scope` uses the `account.schema.json` values, plus `statement`, `gaps` and `full_situation_claim` (true only for all disclosed accounts, complete, no outside assets).
