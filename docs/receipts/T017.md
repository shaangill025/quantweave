## Changes
- `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/reconciliation.py` (new):
  - `reconcile` compares one `SourceSnapshot`, mapped through `SourceMap`, with the journal reconstruction **as of the snapshot's own time**, using journal knowledge as of the evaluation time.
    - Typed differences: missing position, quantity mismatch, cash mismatch, unknown instrument. Each is classified against a caller `Tolerance` (units, and cash per currency). No tolerance is invented: a currency without one is material (`no_tolerance`), and an unknown instrument is always material.
    - Statuses: `reconciled`, `immaterial_difference`, `material_conflict`, `stale` (account events effective after the snapshot, listed in `unreconciled_events`, or snapshot older than `max_age`), `incomplete`. All but the first two block sizing.
    - It never writes the journal; corrections stay explicit `Journal.reverse/correct` calls.
    - The result records the snapshot ref, source kind, observed time (the snapshot's as-of, never its receipt time), ingestion delay, observation age, cash basis and the journal revision as of the evaluation time.
  - `cash_states` per (account, currency) gives settled, unsettled credits, unsettled debits, reported (from the reconciled observation), external encumbrances, pending withdrawals, `available` and `spendable`, with provenance (`event:`, `order:`, `withdrawal:`, `snapshot:` refs) and `included` components.
    - A trade settles at the session close of the `lag`-th trading day after its local trade date. `SettlementTerms` (calendar and lag) come from the caller per instrument; no regulation is encoded (spec §4).
    - `available` = settled + unsettled debits − open-order encumbrances. Unsettled sale credits never count (AT049). `spendable` = `available` − pending withdrawals.
    - A reversed trade and its reversal are both left out. Missing settlement terms, a calendar gap, a buy order with no hold or limit, or disagreeing copies of one order leave that currency's figures `None` with a reason, never zero.
  - `OpenOrder` is a recorded fact (user-declared or snapshot-reported), never submitted. One order ref counts once. A buy holds its reported hold, else quantity × limit + fee reserve, rounded up. A sell holds units (`unit_availability`) and no cash. Expired or not-yet-known orders are ignored.
  - `risk_account_inputs` and `with_account_state` fill `RiskInputs.account_observed_at`, `reconciled`, `available_cash` and `pending_withdrawals`:
    - newer unreconciled events clear `account_observed_at`, and an age-only stale snapshot keeps its as-of time for the risk age check (both → `ACCOUNT_STALE`);
    - only `material_conflict` and `incomplete` set `reconciled=False` (→ `ACCOUNT_CONFLICT`);
    - a user-reported or mirror basis, a missing cash state or unknown cash leave `available_cash` unset (→ `CASH_UNCONFIRMED`).
    - `available_cash` is `available`, not `spendable`, because `risk.evaluate` subtracts pending withdrawals itself; passing `spendable` would subtract them twice.

## Negative/adverse cases
- Oracles: NUM01 (cash 738 after both trades settle; 499 settled with 239 unsettled before the sale settles), NUM06, and hand-computed values (holiday then early close at 18:00 UTC; hold 120.5 and withdrawal 200 giving available 378.5 and spendable 178.5).
- Hypothesis properties (derandomized, 60 examples each):
  - the snapshot built from the journal matches an independent running-sum oracle of units and cash, and has no differences;
  - a stale snapshot leaves journal entries and journal-derived cash unchanged and is `stale` exactly when newer events exist;
  - available ≤ settled and spendable ≤ available.
- Knowledge versus effective time: a trade recorded after the snapshot but effective before it, and a backdated reversal posted later, are `material_conflict`; an event effective exactly at the snapshot time is compared, not newer; two newer events are both listed as `stale`.
- Rejections: negative `max_age`, snapshot after as-of, unmapped account, unknown source, negative or mis-keyed tolerance.
- Mutation controls (worker self-report, scratchpad-only, not reproduced by the reviewer), each applied alone to the pre-review revision; all 21 were caught: unsettled credits counted; debits ignored; encumbrances ignored; stale-by-newer-events off; stale-by-age off; immediate settlement; missing terms treated as settled; inclusive tolerance; missing tolerance immaterial; unknown instrument judged by tolerance; unit tolerance ignored; incomplete treated as complete; order dedupe off; manual source as broker cash; receipt time as observed time; withdrawals netted twice; reversal pair kept; stale observed time kept; expiry off; sell orders not encumbering units; omitted cash in a complete snapshot skipped.
