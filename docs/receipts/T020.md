## PR A — scoped pauses and unitised drawdown
Files: `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/risk_pauses.py` (188 lines), `packages/domain/tests/test_risk_pauses.py` (112 lines; 5 tests, 10 cases), this receipt.
- `PauseBook` is an append-only chain of `Pause` and `Resume` events, and state is derived from it. Every construction, including a direct `PauseBook(...)`, replays and validates the whole chain:
  - each resume follows an earlier, unresumed pause of the same id;
  - ids are unique;
  - the resume time is not before the trigger;
  - a material pause, or one caused by stale or unreconciled data, carries a reconciled-revision id;
  - a tenant-kind scope names the book's own tenant (`tenant_scope`); a mismatch is rejected, not ignored.
- Scopes are tenant (= "all" for the book's tenant), account, sleeve and strategy. Triggers: drawdown, planned loss, loss limit, stale data, reconciliation flag, user.
- Nothing clears a pause but an explicit `Resume`: no expiry, no midnight reset, no deposit. A resume needs a principal id, a reason, a step-up receipt id and an aware time (normalised to UTC).
  - Only the **form** of the step-up and reconciled-revision ids is checked here. Verifying them against T010 step-up records and a fresh T017 reconciled revision is remaining work.
- `unitized_drawdown`: D = 1 - V/H on unit values, rounded up. `drawdown_breached` is D >= limit.
- Reading of §7, applied in PR B: while a pause is active, buys and sales of stock that covers a short call are blocked. Other sells (checked reductions) still run every check, and a pause never liquidates anything.

## PR B (stacked) — the risk evaluator
Files: `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/risk.py` (560 lines), `packages/domain/tests/test_risk.py` (502 lines; 17 tests, 49 cases). PR B totals 1062 lines: under the 1200-line limit, above the 900-line target.
- **Dependency gap (T017 incomplete).** Cash availability (net of commitments), allocation usage, account freshness and reconciliation are explicit caller inputs (`RiskInputs`). A missing value blocks the action (`CASH_UNCONFIRMED`, `ACCOUNT_STALE`, `ACCOUNT_CONFLICT`); none is defaulted.
- **Completeness contract.** The caller must supply every holding in scope: `held_units` for the account and `position_values` in the scope currency.
  - A held instrument other than the traded one with no value blocks (`EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`).
  - The traded instrument is valued at units x the fresh mark. If the caller also lists a value, it must agree within `value_tolerance`, a caller parameter that the spec does not set; `None` means exact. Otherwise the action blocks.
- `evaluate(history, action, inputs, pauses)` returns `pass`/`warn`/`block`, typed `RiskCode` reasons, and one `LimitCheck` per limit. Each `LimitCheck` gives the value, threshold, denominator name and amount, ratio, headroom, basis and provenance.
- Gates:
  - `check_sizing` (T019);
  - `horizon:` exclusions;
  - leveraged, inverse or unknown instruments for buys (R044);
  - scoped pauses;
  - account freshness and reconciliation;
  - cash and allocation usage;
  - mark presence, freshness and currency; a buy needs an ask or last mark (`MARK_UNSUITABLE`);
  - denominators, and FX freshness and pair direction (`FX_UNAVAILABLE`);
  - wrong-currency exposures, position values or flows (`CURRENCY_MISMATCH`, typed, not raised);
  - exposures, held units and stress shocks.
- Limits: cash reserve, the policy allocation (R010), issuer, sector and position concentration, planned trade loss, and stress loss. A ratio limit uses its own denominator after pending withdrawals and the action's costs. Pending deposits never add cash. Withdrawals reduce cash and every denominator, and `WITHDRAWAL_RESERVE_BREACH` blocks the dependent buy.
- Feasible size: each check is affine in q (exact `Fraction`) and gives an interval of passing sizes. Their intersection is floored to the lot; 0 means none. A breach blocks and reports `alternative` only when every reason is size-dependent. Nothing is silently resized.
- Sells are long-only. **Interpretation** of §5 "Risk-reducing proposals still pass full checks": a sell passes a check only if the post-trade measure stays within the limit, or the post-trade measure (costs included, ratio for ratio limits) is not above the pre-trade one. Selling stock that covers a short call is never a reduction.
  - Where the two passing regions are disjoint, the higher one is used. That is a safe subset, so the feasible size can be conservative.
- Stop-gap reading: no spec text defines "stop-gap". Following §5 ("Stops do not guarantee fills") and NUM07 (`guaranteed_maximum: false`), planned loss uses the lowest of the stop and any caller-supplied gap exit prices.
  - Without a gap scenario the result warns `stop_fill_assumed_no_gap_scenario`.
  - A missing or non-positive per-unit loss abstains.
  - Stress shocks come from the caller only.
