### Rules (method `sector_rank_quality_value/1`)
- **Point in time.** Facts come only from `FactBook.as_of(t, basis)`, with publication or receipt basis. The classification used is the latest one known at or before t; tied different records are unknown. The market value must be observed at or before t; one observed later counts as absent. Its age must be within `market_max_age`.
- **Recipe (general corporate).** Annual (350–380 day) us-gaap facts in the issuer's reporting currency. All of a metric's inputs are read at one period end: the latest annual end of its first flow concept. The metrics and their formulas:

  | Metric | Formula | Better |
  |---|---|---|
  | operating margin | OI/Revenue | higher |
  | pre-tax ROIC | OI/(Equity + LongTermDebt) | higher |
  | cash conversion | (CFO − capex)/NI | higher |
  | debt/assets | LongTermDebt/Assets | lower |
  | operating earnings/EV | OI/(MV + debt − cash) | higher |
  | FCF yield | (CFO − capex)/MV | higher |

- **Sector compatibility.** Each metric declares the issuer classes it is valid for. Following §8, this recipe is valid only for `general_corporate`. Banks, insurers, funds and pre-revenue issuers get `not_applicable` on every metric, with no number, no score and no rank, and they are not peers.
- **Negative earnings.** A non-positive denominator makes the metric `undefined` with a reason. This covers revenue, invested capital, NI, assets and EV. Negative capex is `undefined` (`capex_negative`) for cash conversion and FCF yield, so it never raises FCF. For the valuation yields, OI ≤ 0 or FCF ≤ 0 is `undefined` (the positive-denominator EV/EBIT and P/FCF rule), never a cheap multiple.
- **Stale filings.** The metric is `stale` when the period end is more than `max_fact_age_days` before t. A newer filing published after t is not seen.
- **Missingness.** Missing is `missing` with the concept and date. A tied restatement with a different value is `conflicted`. None of these states is zero or imputed.
- **Scoring.** Within a sector, among peers with a value, the score is the percentile (worse + ties/2)/(n − 1) in the metric's better direction. Below `min_peers` the metric is unscored. Composite = weighted mean of the scored metrics. Coverage = scored weight / applicable weight. Below `min_coverage` (or with nothing scored) the issuer is `insufficient_coverage`. Ranks run within the sector by composite, ties by canonical instrument id. Arithmetic is exact (`Fraction`) and shown half-even at 1e-18.
- **Manifest.** STR-QUALITY-001 `0.1.0-research`, long-term, stocks only. Its code hash is the module source. The parameters are bounded:

  | Parameter | Default | Range |
  |---|---|---|
  | `max_fact_age_days` | 456 | 90–1095 |
  | `min_peers` | 3 | 2–1000 |
  | `min_coverage` | 0.5 | 0–1 |
  | `weight_<metric>` (each) | 1 | 0–10 |

  Parameters are read only from a config whose hash equals the adopted `config_hash`. Otherwise the run is blocked with `config_mismatch` or `config_invalid`. All-zero weights block the run with `config_invalid:weights`. A market value keyed under another instrument is refused at input construction. Registered in `StrategyRegistry`, the manifest is refused by the T027 `actionable` gate (capability missing, operational not passed).

## Interpretations (owner review)
- **Defaults.** Neither the spec nor the catalogue gives a staleness age, peer minimum, coverage floor or weights. The defaults are unqualified research choices. 456 days is a 12-month fiscal year plus about 90 days to file.
- **Debt and EV (approximate).** EV = MV + LongTermDebt − cash, and invested capital = equity + LongTermDebt. Both use `LongTermDebt` only and ignore current debt, leases, preferred stock and minority interest. An issuer that does not tag `LongTermDebt` reads as missing, not zero, which lowers its coverage.
- **Ranks.** Percentile ranks replace winsorization: they are bounded and outlier-robust, which is the spec's "explicit winsorization/rank rules" in rank form.

## Negative/adverse cases
- **Hand oracles.** A, B and C hold six exact metric values (for example 2/21 for EBIT/EV). Percentiles are 0, 1/2 and 1, composites 1/2, 0 and 1, and ranks are C, A, B. Equal issuers tie at 1/4 and are ordered by id.
- **Negative earnings.** NI −50, OI −20 and FCF −30 make three metrics `undefined` with reasons. The negative margin −1/45 is a real worst rank. Valuation peers are unchanged.
- **Incompatible classes.** Bank, insurer, fund and pre-revenue are all `not_applicable`, and they do not affect peers' scores. A reclassification known after t is not applied at t. A classification known after t is `classification_unknown`.
- **Stale.** The boundary is exact: day 456 is a value, day 457 is `stale`. A FY2026 10-K published after t is ignored until its publication time.
- **Missing.** A missing debt fact makes three metrics `missing`, giving coverage 1/2 and a hand composite of 1/4. A `min_coverage` of 0.6 makes the issuer `insufficient_coverage`.
- **Market value.** A missing, future, stale or other-currency market value blocks only the two valuation metrics.
- **Other cases.** A peer group below the minimum, a tied conflicting restatement, a config hash mismatch and an out-of-bounds config are all tested.
- **Review fixes.** Each fix had a failing test before the fix: a swapped market-value key (it used to give FCF yield 1.2 instead of 0.06), negative capex and all-zero weights. Removing each fix makes its test fail again.
- **Property (150 examples, differential).** Facts, classifications and market values get random known times around t, under both knowledge bases. The result equals the run on inputs pre-filtered to what was known at t, with the universe and classifications reversed (so it is order-independent). Every issuer classified as incompatible at t is `not_applicable` on every metric. The property shows only that no input unknown at t is used. Value correctness rests on the hand-computed tests.
- **Mutation controls.** Twelve mutants were applied to a working copy, and the restore was verified by `diff`. Each was killed by at least one test:
  - a `!= 0` denominator;
  - no sector check;
  - `>=` stale;
  - ignored direction;
  - accepting a future market value;
  - zero-imputing missing;
  - reading facts 60 days ahead;
  - ignoring the classification's known time;
  - no OI ≤ 0 guard;
  - no coverage floor;
  - a rank counter stuck at 1;
  - ascending composite order.
