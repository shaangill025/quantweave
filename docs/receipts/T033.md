## Part B: walk-forward backtest, ledger binding, claims

Changes:
- `qw_domain/backtest.py` (new): hashed price series; `CostModel` (total rate in [0, 1)); baselines (buy-and-hold or cash); `EvaluationProtocol` with a structured `DeclaredClaim` (metric, direction, baseline; wire field `hypothesis`); `PitContext`; `walk_forward`; `run_trial`; `trial_report`/`ledger_report`; `claim_for`; `evaluation_receipt`.
- `qw_domain/trial_ledger.py`: `SplitPlan.protocol_hash` is required, and `Trial.protocol_hash` must equal it (`protocol_changed`).
- `tests/test_backtest.py` (new): 10 test functions, including one Hypothesis property (25 examples).
- `tests/test_trial_ledger.py`: the fixtures carry a protocol hash.

Rules:
- **Chronology**: only a granted train/development access is evaluated, and `open_view` re-checks rights, tenant and manifest. Promotion access is refused (`promotion_protocol`). Only bars inside the access window are used. The protocol must hash to the plan's registered `protocol_hash`, and every trial records it.
- **Point in time**: at each bar's knowledge time the strategy sees the bars known by then and the split view. A later time or later inputs are refused (`look_ahead`).
- **Fills and costs**: the fill is at the open of bar i + lag, with lag ≥ 1 and that open strictly after the decision (`same_bar_fill`). cost = |w − drifted previous weight| × (commission + half spread + slippage). net = w·R − cost, and net ≤ −1 is refused (`total_loss`). Weights lie in [0, 1].
- **Claims**:
  - `claim_for` and `evaluation_receipt` take the ledger.
  - They require a completed trial bound to the evaluation's access and protocol (`trial_not_recorded`).
  - The trial's recorded metrics must equal the evaluation's, and the evaluation's periods must reproduce its summary (`metrics_mismatch`).
  - The trial report and `trial_count` come from the ledger.
  - `significance_claimed` is always false and the evidence ceiling is `limited` (`development_window_not_promotion`, plus `synthetic_only`). No `EvidenceRecord` is produced.

Negative cases (SYNTHETIC):
- **Hand-computed backtest** (opens 100, 110, 99, 99, 108.9; weights 1, 0, 1; cost 0.002): net −0.102, −0.002, 0.098, mean −0.002. Buy-and-hold excess mean −0.004/3. Drift 11/21, rebalance cost 0.002/42.
- **Refusals**:
  - a peek at future facts, and a decision with future inputs (also recorded as a FAILED trial);
  - lag 0, bars known after the next open, and a promotion access;
  - a zero-cost protocol under the same plan, a re-dated protocol, and a trial with another protocol hash (`protocol_changed`);
  - a holdout instrument, a misaligned baseline, an evidence-class mismatch, a free-text claim, and an excess claim on an undeclared baseline;
  - a total cost rate ≥ 1, a total-loss period, an unregistered run, and a trial count below the number of reported trials;
  - review probes for `metrics_mismatch`: an evaluation whose summary mean was edited to 0.05, in both the claim and the receipt; edited periods; and a self-consistent but unrecorded forgery.
  - bars past the window are excluded.
- **Property**: for any outcomes of extra trials plus one unrecorded access, the receipt lists every ledger trial and counts the unrecorded one. Its claim has `trial_count` = trials + 1, CI keys, `significance_claimed` false and no "edge". The report is the same for any shuffled input order.
- **Mutation controls** (worker self-report, each reverted): 21/21 killed.

Gaps and disclosed limitations:
- Domain-level and partial; no acceptance scenario has been run end to end. AT040, AT059, AT065 and AT084 are partial; AT024, AT029, AT062 and AT099 are not addressed.
- **Price series are not bound to the hashed dataset or its rights gate.** Only tenant, evidence class, instrument and window are checked. T032 price records must exist before any non-synthetic use.
- **The look-ahead guard is cooperative.** A strategy closure that holds other data is not detected.
- **No liquidation cost** is charged at the end of the window.
- **Baseline results are not re-verified** by `claim_for`.
- One instrument per evaluation; no shorting, borrow, partial fills or volume limits; times are supplied by the caller.
- The real controls against an edge badge are `significance_claimed=false`, the `limited` ceiling and the ledger-sourced trial count.
