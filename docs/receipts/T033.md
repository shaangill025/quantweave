## Part B: walk-forward backtest, ledger binding, claims

Changes:
- `qw_domain/backtest.py` (new): hashed price series; `CostModel` (total rate in [0, 1)); baselines (buy-and-hold or cash); `EvaluationProtocol` with a structured `DeclaredClaim` (metric, direction, baseline; wire field `hypothesis`); `PitContext`; `walk_forward`; `run_trial`; `trial_report`/`ledger_report`; `claim_for`; `evaluation_receipt`.
- `qw_domain/trial_ledger.py`: `SplitPlan.protocol_hash` is required, and `Trial.protocol_hash` must equal it (`protocol_changed`).
- `tests/test_backtest.py` (new): 10 test functions, including one Hypothesis property (25 examples).
- `tests/test_trial_ledger.py`: the fixtures carry a protocol hash.

Rules:
- **Chronology**: only a granted train/development access is evaluated, and `open_view` re-checks rights, tenant and manifest. Promotion access is refused (`promotion_protocol`). Only bars inside the access window are used. The protocol must hash to the plan's registered `protocol_hash`, and every trial records it.
- **Point in time**: at each bar's knowledge time the strategy sees the bars known by then and the split view. A later time or later inputs are refused (`look_ahead`).
- **Fills and costs**: the fill is at the open of bar i + lag, with lag ≥ 1 and that open strictly after the decision (`same_bar_fill`). cost = |w − drifted previous weight| × (commission + half spread + slippage). net = w·R − cost, and net ≤ −1 is refused (`total_loss`). Weights lie in [0, 1].
- **Claims**:
  - `claim_for` and `evaluation_receipt` take the ledger.
  - They require a completed trial bound to the evaluation's access and protocol (`trial_not_recorded`).
  - The trial's recorded metrics must equal the evaluation's, and the evaluation's periods must reproduce its summary (`metrics_mismatch`).
  - The trial report and `trial_count` come from the ledger.
  - `significance_claimed` is always false and the evidence ceiling is `limited` (`development_window_not_promotion`, plus `synthetic_only`). No `EvidenceRecord` is produced.

## Part C: prospective shadow capture, and the Part B baseline follow-up

Branch `t033-shadow-capture`, base `536b3d6`; written by a worker that did not commit. Stdlib-only domain code on SYNTHETIC data. No dependency, network, provider, broker or billable call, and no order code. Read: spec §14 (three separate records; "Prospective capture uses frozen ... versions and timestamps decisions before outcomes"; separate dashboards), §17 ("Prospective decisions must be frozen before outcomes"), §8 (prospective observations; evidence enum; no universal thresholds), `common.schema.json` evidence enum, AT024/029/040/059/062/065/084/099. A pre-review draft from the scratchpad was used for ideas only. Its outcome records were replaced by scoring from bars. Backlog status not changed.

Changes:
- `qw_domain/shadow.py` (new): `open_run`, `ShadowRun`, `ShadowDecision`, `ShadowBook`, `score`, `ShadowReport`.
- `qw_domain/backtest.py` (follow-up b):
  - `_metrics` now records `baseline.<id>.net_mean`, `baseline.<id>.excess_mean` and the excess interval bounds for every baseline.
  - `claim_for` compares them with the trial's recorded metrics, and checks that each baseline's summary and excess follow from its periods and the protocol's baselines (`metrics_mismatch`).
  - Baseline ids are capped at 96 characters so the metric keys stay valid ids.
- `tests/test_shadow.py` (new): 7 test functions. `tests/test_backtest.py`: forged-baseline probes, plus the id-length guard.

Rules (shadow):
- **Binding.** `open_run` needs:
  - a version in the strategy registry;
  - a trial in the T032 ledger whose material and config hashes equal that version's under the given configuration (`candidate_mismatch`);
  - the protocol whose hash the trial and plan recorded (`protocol_changed`);
  - a start time no earlier than the trial and the protocol declaration (`not_frozen`);
  - a declared `min_scored` ≥ 1.

  Every decision carries the run id and the protocol hash.
- **Point in time, append-only.**
  - Inputs must be known by `decided_at` (`look_ahead`), and no decision may precede the run (`before_registration`).
  - Capture must fall within `[decided_at, decided_at + max_capture_delay]` (`backfill`) and in time order (`time_order`).
  - An identical re-capture is a no-op. The same id with any other field, including a new `captured_at`, is `conflicting_duplicate`, so a retry cannot reset timestamps.
  - Construction replays the whole book.
- **Scoring.**
  - Only bars known by `as_of` on a declared grid are used, and every bar must sit on the grid (`off_grid`).
  - A decision belongs to the last grid bar ended by `decided_at`. It fills, as in `backtest`, at the open of the bar `fill_lag_bars` later, which must start after the decision; otherwise it is a late decision.
  - A slot is due once its fill bar has started by `as_of`; before that, its decisions are pending.
  - Returns, turnover and cost use the backtest formulas and the protocol's `CostModel`.
  - A missing or not-yet-known bar makes the slot `unavailable`, never zero.
  - The run starts flat. After any unscored slot the held weight is unknown, so turnover is charged at the worst case max(w, 1 − w) (`gap_turnover_worst_case`).
  - Historical bars are refused (`not_prospective`).
- **Reporting.**
  - Counts: due, scored, abstained, missed, unavailable, late, superseded, pending, unscheduled.
  - Intervals are `eval_stats` Newey-West.
  - Baselines are scored on the same slots; buy-and-hold re-buys after a gap.
  - `record_kind: prospective_shadow`, with no backtest figures (no dataset, split or window fields). Nothing combines it with an `Evaluation`.
  - `significance_claimed` is always false, and the report never mentions an edge.
- **Evidence ceiling.**
  - The spec sets no universal minimum (§14: "Do not hardcode one universal ... number of trades or days"). The run therefore declares `min_scored` at registration, inside the declaration hash (see the review fixes for what that hash does and does not prove).
  - Below that minimum the ceiling is `none`, `claim_permitted` is false and the limitation is `below_declared_minimum`.
  - Otherwise synthetic bars cap the ceiling at `limited` (`synthetic_only`), and prospective bars at `prospective_limited`. The report never reaches `qualified_for_declared_claim`.

AT specs applied (partial, domain level; none run end to end):
- AT040: synthetic, historical and prospective evidence are kept apart; historical replay is refused as a shadow.
- AT084: every forward decision is tracked point in time, including late, superseded, pending and missed ones.
- AT059: a failed or unqualified variant can be shadowed without any edge claim.
- AT065: the ceiling sits on a separate axis and needs the declared minimum.
- AT024: the version, configuration and protocol are bound, so a changed version does not inherit a shadow record.
- AT029, AT062, AT099: not addressed.

### Review fixes (round 1)

The reviewer (a different model) returned CHANGES REQUESTED with probes in the scratchpad. Each fix below has a test that I confirmed fails when that fix alone is temporarily reverted.

- **S1, hindsight capture.**
  - A decision is scored only if it was both decided and captured before its fill bar opens and by `as_of`; otherwise it counts as `late_decisions` or `late_captured`.
  - Supersession considers only such decisions, so a backdated decision recorded later cannot win.
  - `max_capture_delay` must be below the grid's minimum start-to-start spacing; the 60-day probe is refused.
  - Test: decided 1 min before the bar 1 open and captured 3 min after it, weight 0. It is not scored, `sd-1` keeps net −0.102 (late_captured 1, superseded 0), and alone the slot is `missed`.
- **S2, grid.**
  - The grid is declared in `open_run`. It is validated there: Windows, chronological, non-overlapping, and at least `fill_lag_bars + 2` bars.
  - The grid is carried in the run, hashed (`grid_hash`, in `to_wire`) and used by `score`, which no longer takes a grid.
  - A grid with bar 2 removed changes the declaration hash and is refused (`declaration_mismatch`). Series bars off the declared grid are still `off_grid`.
  - Gaps between consecutive windows (nights, weekends) are allowed. Only the declared grid defines the slots.
- **S3, declaration.**
  - `ShadowRun.declaration_hash` covers run id, tenant, strategy, version, candidate hashes, trial id, protocol hash, instrument, `min_scored`, `max_capture_delay`, grid hash and `registered_at`, and appears in `to_wire`.
  - `score` requires the hash recorded at registration (`declaration_mismatch`). A `replace(run, min_scored=1)` made after seeing results is refused when the hash recorded at registration is supplied; a caller who supplies the replaced run's own hash is not caught until the declaration is persisted with a server timestamp, which is required before any real shadow run.
  - **Limit, stated plainly:** with no persistence, the hash proves only that the scored run equals the declaration the caller recorded. It cannot prove when that declaration was recorded, or that it preceded the outcomes. That needs a persisted, server-timestamped registration (deferred).
- **N1, baseline chaining.** Buy-and-hold is now chained over every due slot with its own bar availability, then compared on common slots. Test: decisions 1, abstain, 1 give baseline nets −0.102, 0, 0.10 against strategy −0.102 and 0.098 on slots 0 and 2, so excess is 0 and −0.002, mean −0.001.
- **N3, pending outcomes.** A new `pending_outcome` status covers a period-end bar that has not ended by `as_of` or lies beyond the grid. An ended but missing or unknown bar stays `unavailable`. The full-capture slot 3 and the early-`as_of` slot 1 are now `pending_outcome`.
- **N4, `claim_for` checks.**
  - (i) Truncated baseline periods give `metrics_mismatch` instead of a raw `ValueError`.
  - (ii) `_consistent` checks net = gross − cost and cost = turnover × rate in every strategy and baseline period. It also recomputes `gross_mean` and `total_cost` from the periods.
  - Each baseline's `total_cost` is now a recorded metric. Without it, a baseline period claiming zero turnover and zero cost, with gross equal to net, would pass the arithmetic.
  - All four of the reviewer's p3 probes now raise `metrics_mismatch`, plus a gross ±0.1 swap that keeps `gross_mean`.
- **N6.** The module docstring now explains that slot assignment uses the bar window `end`, not `known_at`, and that `inputs_known_at` is caller-declared, so the look-ahead guard is cooperative.
