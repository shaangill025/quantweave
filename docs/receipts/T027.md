### Rules
- **Manifest versions are immutable**: re-registering a (strategy, version) with different content is refused; identical content is a no-op. Collection fields (parameters, data, asset classes, uses, allowed values) are copied to tuples/frozensets at construction, so caller mutation cannot change a hash; parameters hash in name order.
- **Material change**: `material_hash` covers id, family, implementation hash, model config, data needs, asset classes, horizon, regular-session flag, every parameter declaration (bounds, allowed values, defaults) and research protocol. Only `title` and the version label are outside it. Axes and adoption key on the material hash, so a material change reads as not tested / no evidence / not evaluated / stale adoption until requalified and re-adopted. A display-only change carries both. The user's earlier adopted version stays adopted (a maintainer's new version does not change it, R034).
- **Configuration**: unknown names, floats, `bool` for integers, non-finite decimals and out-of-bounds values are refused. Decimal values hash by value (`0.050` = `0.05`). Every configuration value is treated as investment-affecting (§7 "exposed combinations do not inherit validation"; §13): evidence and eligibility key on (material hash, config hash), and adoption binds both.
- **Axes**: operational `not_tested → passed|failed`, `passed → passed|suspended|failed`, `suspended → passed|failed`, `failed` terminal for that material hash. Evidence climbs one step at a time (`none → limited|historical`, `historical → prospective_limited → qualified_for_declared_claim`) and can be downgraded. Eligibility moves freely after its first evaluation and is per tenant. Each step needs an actor id, at least one evidence record known at the step, and time order. Synthetic-only evidence can reach at most `limited`, and the status flags it; nothing in this increment authorises action. `failed` being final for a material hash is a deliberate policy: a fix is a new manifest version. Evidence refs are opaque ids, not yet bound to material hash, configuration or tenant; increment 2 verifies them against stored evidence.
- **Predeclared family policy**: an evidence step above `none` needs a family policy approved at or before the step and records its hash; a `limited` step is refused unless that policy permits limited evidence. `limited_evidence_permitted` reflects the recorded policy only; the increment-2 gate must also evaluate the policy current at decision time.
- **Adoption**: explicit consent with principal and step-up ids; the acknowledgement text is stored and never authorises. Receipts per (tenant, strategy) must be signed in non-decreasing time; `status` reads the latest receipt by `signed_at` at or before `at`. Adoption alone never implies eligibility; the increment-2 gate is the enforcer. Step-up verification against T010 records (bound to tenant and principal) and refusal of step-up reuse are the adopt route's responsibility.
- **Integrity**: like `rights.Registry`, construction replays every family-policy, qualification and adoption history through the method rules and checks keys against registered manifests (material hash, tenant, strategy); forged or reordered records are refused with `integrity`. A stored config hash cannot be re-derived from the hash alone. **Clock precondition**: step `at`, `approved_at`, `signed_at` and the `status` read time come from the server clock. A future-dated step blocks later steps through time order, so persistence must stamp them. All datetimes must be aware and are normalised to UTC; naive values raise `InstantError`.

## Rules
- **Fail closed, every reason, in order**: both gates collect all applicable reasons and sort them in the §7 order: unknown strategy / invalid configuration (these stop evaluation: nothing else can be keyed), jurisdiction (a `check_use` jurisdiction denial, reported as `jurisdiction_not_permitted` so it sorts first), feature permission (investment policy adopted at `at`, not SYNTHETIC, receipt matching its version and tenant, AI mode for ai_thesis), instrument/catalogue (policy instruments and horizons; options are refused when the policy has a `new_option_ideas` exclusion or every allocated account carries an `option_proposals:<account>` exclusion, which `build_draft` records for unknown or denied option permission; intraday only when regular-session-only, family capabilities plus option terms when options are declared), qualification (operational `passed`; evidence; family policy; eligibility for this tenant; evidence refs verified), adoption (current, bound to the same material and config hash), source rights (`rights.check_use` for every declared use of every `DataNeed`, plus `financial_recommendation` for action and `model_processing` for ai_thesis).
- **Evidence sufficiency (interpretation, fail closed)**: only `qualified_for_declared_claim` is sufficient on its own. `limited`, `historical` and `prospective_limited` are limited evidence and need the family policy in force at `at` to permit it. The evidence must have been assessed under that same policy: a newer approved policy (even a permitting one) gives `family_policy_superseded` until requalification. A policy approved after `at` is not yet known, so it neither permits nor supersedes (increment-1 review nit b). Synthetic-only evidence is never actionable.
- **Research-only is strictly weaker**: `research_eligible` needs a valid configuration, operational state not `failed`/`suspended` (`not_tested` allowed), verified operational evidence refs if a step exists, rights for declared uses, and for ai_thesis an adopted `ai_enabled` policy and `model_processing`. It needs no evidence, eligibility, adoption, policy instruments/horizons, capabilities or `financial_recommendation`. `actionable` runs the same checks plus the rest, so actionable implies research-eligible.
- **Evidence refs**: each ref of the latest operational, evidence and eligibility step known at `at` must resolve in the supplied index to a record bound to the same key (material hash; plus config hash; plus tenant) with the same synthetic flag and recorded time. Missing or mismatched records give `evidence_unverified`.
- **Decision time**: steps, adoption receipts, policy receipts (latest signed at or before `at`), family policies and rights are all read as known at `at`, which must be the server clock.

## Gaps
- Evidence records are a caller-supplied index (no evidence store yet; T036 territory); capabilities are caller-asserted facts.
- Per-proposal checks are not here: session open (`OUTSIDE_SESSION`), freshness, risk pauses, sizing, option contract terms per contract. Account-level option exclusions are applied here only as "no allocated account permits options"; choosing a permitted account per proposal is the proposal gate's job.
- A malformed evidence index (a naive `recorded_at`, a non-`EvidenceRecord` value) raises rather than returning a typed denial; it fails closed.
- `PolicyHistory` is not replayed: like the other caller inputs it is trusted after the tenant, version, receipt-hash and content-hash checks.

## Interpretations (owner decisions)
- `family_policy_superseded` also denies `qualified_for_declared_claim` evidence until requalification under the newer family policy. This is stricter than §8, which constrains only limited-evidence action. Owner decision: keep, or restrict it to limited evidence.
- `research_eligible` needs an adopted investment policy only for ai_thesis (AI mode). The other families may run research with no adopted policy, reading §7's ordering and the catalogue's "research starting points" as research needing no investment authority.
- Review item: jurisdiction denials were first reported as `source_rights_denied`, sorting last. Fixed by mapping them to `jurisdiction_not_permitted`, the first §7 check.
- R034 "after release approval": maintainer release approval is not modelled. Changing `-0` hashing changes the material hash of any manifest that declares a `-0` bound or default (none are persisted).
