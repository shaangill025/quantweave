## Changes (4 code files + this receipt)
- **`qw_domain/option_risk.py`**
  - **`contract_terms`** reduces a T011 `OptionContract` to N underlying units plus cash C, against a strike payment K×M per contract. The effective strike is K′ = (K×M − C)/N. Nothing defaults to 100.
    - Terms that are unverified (including adjusted terms not yet verified) or unknown return `Unavailable`. The code is the first reason from `sizing_block_reasons`, and the reason text lists all of them. Unknown terms include the multiplier, the deliverables, cash in lieu, the exercise style and the settlement.
    - A deliverable without units of the underlying (for example cash only), baskets, cash in a foreign currency, a negative cash component and a non-positive K′ also return `Unavailable`.
  - **`expiry_intrinsic`** follows the spec 09 payoff convention for each long contract. Adjusted deliverables use the reduction above.
  - **`check_cover`** checks the open state, netted by contract, for one account and one currency (R043: another currency returns `Unavailable`). It applies the fully secured convention from spec 09:
    - Premium paid is spent. Premium received never funds anything, because it is unsettled.
    - A short put reserves the gross K×M.
    - A short call encumbers N held units and also reserves the cash component C. Units cover only one call. A cash-settled short call is uncovered.
    - A short paired with a long of the same right, expiry and terms reserves the width of the strike payment. The long must be at least as exercisable as the short: an American long covers either style, and a European long covers only a European short. Other pairs stay unpaired.
    - Among admissible pairings, greedy pairing can over-reserve the expiry loss but never under-reserve it.
    - Premiums are counted per `check_cover` call. Every paid premium among the legs is spent, and premium received on closing legs is not netted. This is conservative.
    - `AccountCover.units` is not reduced by other encumbrances, such as other proposals or structures. The caller must pass units that are already net of them.
    - Broker permissions come from the caller. Unknown or missing permissions block.
    - Broker margin formulas are not invented.
  - **`check_legging`** checks every intermediate state, including from existing legs, and blocks if any state blocks. It reports the worst state.
  - **`vertical_bounds`** gives the idealized max loss and max gain from spec 09, including fees.
    - Debit or credit comes from the structure, not from the premium sign. A call spread long the lower strike, or a put spread long the higher strike, is a debit structure.
    - Max loss and max gain then use the actual net premium.
    - Premiums and fees must be in the strike currency and fees must be ≥ 0; otherwise it returns a typed `Unavailable`.
    - Costs round up and gains round down.
- **`qw_domain/decimal_math.py`** provides `exp`, `ln` and `sqrt`, using `decimal`'s correctly rounded operations at 40 digits.
  - `norm_cdf` uses the Φ series with guard digits that grow with x². For |x| ≥ 15 it returns exactly 0 or 1, since the tail there is below 4e-51.
  - The stated absolute tolerance is 1e-35.
  - `exp` underflows to 0 below about x = −2.3e6, because Underflow is not trapped. This is tested.
  - Division goes through an explicit context `divide`; `check_no_float` bans the `/` operator.
- Tests: `test_option_risk.py` has 12 tests (22 cases) and `test_decimal_math.py` has 4 tests (10 cases).

## Negative and adverse cases
- Missing multiplier or deliverables, unknown style, and adjusted terms that are not verified each return `Unavailable` and are never sized.
- Two short calls on 100 units are uncovered, and the adjusted call needs all 150 units. A cash shortfall of USD 0.01 on the cash deliverable or the combined put reserve blocks.
- Unknown and missing broker permissions block.
- Cross-currency cash returns `Unavailable`.
- Inputs are rejected for:
  - a float or zero leg quantity;
  - a float into the Decimal functions;
  - ln(0) and sqrt(−1).
- Legging:
  - A bull put credit entered short first blocks at state 0 (5000 against 1000), while the long-first order passes.
  - A bear call credit entered short first is uncovered.
  - Closing the long leg first leaves a naked short and blocks. Buying back the short first passes.
- Vertical bounds:
  - A bear call paid at a net debit of 400 (width 500) gives loss 900 and gain −400.
  - A bull call at a net credit of 400 gives loss −400 and gain 900.
  - A zero-premium bull call loses the fee only.
  - A fee or premium in another currency, or a negative fee, is refused.
- A European long does not cover an American short, so the short reserves the full 5000. An American long does cover a European short.
- A negative cash deliverable and a cash-only deliverable fail closed.

## Increment 2 — Greeks, lifecycle states and assignment funding
Branch `t034-greeks-lifecycle`, base `717e170`. Stdlib-only domain code (`Decimal`, `Fraction`, no float). No submission, exercise or cancellation path. The worker did not commit. Round-1 review fixes are folded in below.

### Changes
- **`option_greeks.py` (new): `bsm`** gives price, delta, gamma, vega and theta per contract.
  - **Strike:** the effective strike K′ from `contract_terms`, scaled by N units per contract. Nothing assumes 100.
  - **Inputs** (`ModelInputs`): the spot as an underlying `Mark` (instrument, currency, source and observed time); volatility, continuous rate and continuous dividend yield (`Ratio`); `valuation_at` (as of) with `max_age`; the contract's expiry `Session` from its calendar; and a `DayCount`.
  - **Time to expiry:** T runs to that session's close, so half days count. The session must be the contract's own (`expiry_session_mismatch`). The old ±1-day cutoff, which could inflate T, is gone. A provider exercise cutoff later than the close is not used.
  - **Results** carry the inputs, year fraction, model, numerical method (`closed_form/decimal_prec_40`) and `applicable_to_style`. That flag is True only for European contracts, and the result is still a model estimate, not an executable price. American contracts get `bsm_european_approx` with the flag False.
  - **Refusals:** `spot_not_positive`, `vol_not_positive`, `contract_expired`, `spot_stale`, `spot_after_as_of`, `mark_instrument_mismatch`, `currency_mismatch`, `effective_strike_not_positive`, and every `contract_terms` refusal. Naive datetimes and non-`Mark` spots raise.
- **`option_lifecycle.py` (new):** analysis and journal states only.
  - **States:** the eight in spec 09, plus closed and expired.
  - **Transition guards** (`_GUARDS`):
    - Close, exercise and assignment are allowed from any non-exception state while contracts are open.
    - Expiry is allowed once.
    - Worthless expiry is allowed after expiry while contracts are open.
    - Settlement follows an exercise or assignment report; reconciliation follows settlement.
    - Conflict is refused after a terminal state, since spec 09 gives none.
    - `exception_resolved` needs an actor and a reference. It returns to the prior state with quantities unchanged, so nothing is released while in exception.
  - **Collateral:** exercised or assigned contracts stay encumbered until reconciliation. Collateral is released on close, worthless expiry or reconciliation.
  - **Replay:** an identical event is a no-op; a reused id with a different payload raises. An earlier `observed_at` is refused.
  - **`expiry_expectation`** takes a settlement `Mark`, `as_of` and `max_age`, and a caller threshold. A threshold must be > 0 (`auto_exercise_rule_invalid`), and in the money ≥ threshold counts as exercised. It returns the unit and cash flows.
  - **`early_assignment_flags`** handles short American contracts and takes marks for the spot and the option (quoted per unit, × M per contract), plus `at` and `max_age`. It flags:
    - `short_in_the_money`;
    - `no_extrinsic_value`, which is the only flag a deep in-the-money put gets (no rate test);
    - `dividend_exceeds_extrinsic` for a call whose `ExDividend` goes ex after `at` and by the expiry session.
- **`option_risk.py`:**
  - `check_mark` is the shared freshness check, built on `ValuationBasis.stale`.
  - `CoverCheck.assignment_funding` is the gross cash needed if paired American shorts are assigned before the long is exercised. An assigned put buys at K_s×M; an assigned call is delivered by exercising the long at K_l×M. It is summed as if all were assigned at once.
  - **It blocks (spec 05).** `assignment_funding_insufficient` applies when available cash is below the funding plus every non-width reserve (paid premiums, put and covered-call cash). This holds in `check_cover` and therefore in every `check_legging` state.
- **Tests:** `test_option_greeks.py` has 7 tests (8 cases), `test_option_lifecycle.py` has 8, and `test_option_risk.py` gains one parametrized test (4 cases). The legging-order test now uses European legs so that funding does not mask the order check.

### Oracles and properties
- **High-precision point** at S = K = 100, T = 1 (ACT/365F), r = 5% and volatility 20%. Price, delta, gamma, vega and theta match to 1e-25. The reference is a scratchpad script with its own erf series and Machin π at 80 digits, sharing no code with `decimal_math`.
- **Double-precision `erf` values:** the K = 95, q = 2% case; Hull's S = 42, K = 40 (4.76 and 0.81); and the SYNTHETIC adjusted contract with K′ = 33.25.
- **Hypothesis** (120 derandomized examples): put-call parity to 1e-20 relative, delta bounds, delta parity, and gamma ≥ 0 and vega ≥ 0 for both rights.
- **Assignment funding** (reviewer's four cases):

| Structure | Funding |
|---|---|
| Bull put | 5000 |
| Bear call | 5500 |
| Bull call debit | 5000 |
| Bear put debit | 4500 |

  Each passes at funding plus paid premium and blocks one cent below, in both `check_cover` and `check_legging`. European legs give 0.
- **Lifecycle on a 3-contract short put:** two assignments in a row; assign 1 then close 2, then settle and reconcile; assign, settle, assign again; and expiry, assign 1, then the rest expire worthless (1 contract stays encumbered until reconciled). Assigning beyond the open quantity is refused.
