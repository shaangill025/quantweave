## Changes
- `packages/domain/src/qw_domain/journal.py` (new)
  - `SourceObservation`: account, `SourceRef` (source id and record id), `observed_at`, `effective_at`, a read-only string payload and a fingerprint. The fingerprint is SHA-256 over account, source, record, effective time and payload; receipt time is excluded. `ObservationLog.observe` returns `posted`, `duplicate` (same fingerprint) or `conflict` (same record with other content). A conflict is kept in `conflicts` and never overwrites.
  - `JournalEvent` is a separate type with `money` (`MoneyPosting`: `BookAccount`, `Money`, optional instrument sub-ledger) and `units` (`UnitPosting`: `UnitAccount`, `InstrumentId`, `Quantity`) (C-12).
    - Each posting set is empty or has at least 2 lines, and at least one set is non-empty.
    - Zero lines are rejected. Money balances per currency (F-01), and units balance per instrument against `unit_clearing` or `corporate_action_clearing` (F-02).
    - Only `Money` and `Quantity` are accepted, so the decimal types reject scale violations without rounding.
    - Security cost needs an instrument.
    - `event_id` is the fingerprint of the source identity plus the economic content.
  - Constructors (spec §4 "Supported economic events"): `deposit`, `withdrawal`, `fee`, `interest`, `dividend` (cash payment), `buy`, `sell` and `split` (forward and reverse).
    - Cost convention (spec §5 "Cost and P&L reference convention", spec §4 "Monetary and unit ledgers", F-03): acquisition fees capitalise into cost (`cost = qty*price + fee`). Disposition fees reduce proceeds.
    - A partial sale takes proportional cost. Spec §5 names no lot-selection method, so this is average cost, `lot_policy = economic_average_cost/1`. The remaining cost rounds up at scale 12, and the allocated cost is the remainder, so total cost is conserved.
    - The raw fee is kept on the event, outside the postings.
    - `sell` rejects selling more than is held (`short_sale_unsupported`: stocks are long-only) and selling units with unknown cost (`cost_unknown`).
    - `split` has unit postings only. It is rejected (`split_fraction`, quarantine) when the new quantity is not exactly representable, for example 100 × 1:3 with an unknown cash in lieu.
  - `Journal`: append-only, keyed by (account, source, record), or (account, "reversal", original id) for a reversal. A re-post with the same content is a no-op. A re-post with other content is a conflict and is not posted.
    - `recorded_at` (knowledge time) is non-decreasing.
    - `reverse` posts a negating event with the original effective time. `correct` posts that reversal, sourced to the correction record, plus the superseding event. The original entry and its `recorded_at` are untouched (F-05).
    - `events(known_as_of)`, `entries(known_as_of)` and `reversal_of(id, known_as_of)` replay any earlier knowledge state.
  - `materialize(events, effective_as_of)`: a pure fold over postings giving per (account, instrument) `Holding(quantity, cost)`, per (account, currency) cash, and realised net P&L.
    - A negative quantity is kept and flagged `unsupported_short` instead of being rejected (spec §4 outside-coverage holdings).
    - No cost posted, or a net-zero cost, gives `cost=None` (unknown), never 0.

  - Re-importing the CSV gives all duplicates and identical positions.
  - A changed D1 row is a conflict in both the observation log and the journal; the original payload is kept.
  - The payload is immutable.
  - An observation cannot be posted as an event.
- Rejections: unbalanced money; USD against CAD (no netting); a single line; an empty event; zero money or unit lines; security cost without an instrument; units unbalanced across instruments; non-positive deposit or withdrawal; negative fee; `Quantity` used as money and `Money` used as units; scale 13; a qty×price product past scale 12; a naive datetime; selling past holdings, with unknown cost or in the wrong currency; a fractional reverse split.
- Corrections: a correction from 10 to 8 units. As of a knowledge time before the correction the position is 10 units at cost 501; now it is 8 units at cost 401. The original entry and its `recorded_at` are kept. Replay is a duplicate, a second different reversal is a conflict, reversing a reversal is rejected, out-of-order knowledge time is rejected, and an unknown link is rejected.
- Hypothesis (`derandomize=True`, 200 examples, journals of 1–14 events over 2 accounts, 2 instruments, USD and CAD, all 8 kinds). Over the 200 examples, kind counts were checked once: buy 449, sell 73, split 78, plus the money-only kinds.
  - Every event balances per commodity, and so does the whole journal.
  - Posting the sequence twice gives identical positions.
  - Reversing any event k gives the positions of the journal without k.
  - A split preserves total cost and qty × price exactly, including the reverse ratios 1:2 and 1:10.
- Mutation controls: each change was applied alone and reverted, and each was caught. Fee not capitalised; disposition fee ignored; disposition fee doubled; money balance check off; unit balance check off; observation fingerprint ignores payload; event fingerprint ignores postings; a conflict overwrites; knowledge-time order unchecked; reversal not negated; split moves the full quantity; realised sign flipped; long-only check off.
