### Rules (method `cash_first_underweight/1`)
- **Inputs.** Consolidated `sources.AccountView`s (one figure per underlying account and item, so a mirror never counts twice), account observation times and max age, a reserve per account, marks with max age, the instruments confirmed unit-priced, and `Tradability` rows. Also the strategy `config` and the adopted `config_hash`: the band is read only from a config whose `allocation_manifest().config_hash` matches (`config_mismatch` otherwise; an out-of-bounds config is `config_invalid`). The band is never a free caller argument.
- **Base.** Capital base = cash of every in-scope account + Σ units × mark, in one currency. It is exact (`Fraction`) and reported rounded down at 1e-12. Target value = weight × base.
- **Free cash.** Free cash = broker-available cash − reserve, floored at 0, per account. User-reported cash is not spendable (same rule as `scopes._spendable`). A missing reserve makes the account's cash unspendable. Cash never funds another account (R043).
- **Order.** Each step picks the largest relative underweight (target − value) / target. Ties go to the canonical instrument id (UUID string). The account is the permitted one allowing the most units, then the lower cost, then the account id. Each (account, instrument) pair buys at most once. Everything is recomputed after each buy.
- **Sizing.** Quantity = the largest lot multiple with quantity × mark ≤ remaining deficit and cost ≤ free cash. Cost = fixed + quantity × (mark + unit cost), rounded up at 1e-12, the same formula as `proposals.cash_requirement` (tested equal). Free cash stays on the 1e-12 grid, so rounding the cost up never exceeds it. A fail-closed guard raises if it ever did.
- **No sales.** No sale proceeds are assumed (R049). After cash is allocated, every targeted instrument more than band × base from its target is a `sale_required` or `funding_required` prerequisite, never a candidate. A holding outside the target set worth more than band × base is `unmanaged_holding`, not a sale.
- **Run-level blocks.** Any of these blocks the whole run, because the base is unknown: an unadopted target set (or one adopted after `as_of`), no accounts, an account that is not `reconciled`, a stale, missing or future account observation, no cash figure (or negative cash, margin state) in the run currency, a config that is invalid or not the adopted one, another currency's cash or units, a short holding, or a held instrument whose mark is missing, stale, future, in another currency, a bid or reference mark (only ask, mid or last), or not confirmed unit-priced. A blocked run returns no base and no candidates.
- **Abstentions.** These abstain only the affected instrument or account: the same mark problems for an unheld target, no permitted account, unspendable cash or a missing reserve, insufficient cash, a deficit below one lot, or a buy below the minimum notional.
- **Manifest.** STR-ALLOC-001 `0.1.0-research`: family allocation, long-term, stocks/ETFs. Its code hash is the module's sha256. Its only parameter is `drift_band` (decimal in [0, 1], default 0, i.e. no tolerance). The code hash covers all of `allocation.py`: any source edit is a material change needing a new version, requalification and re-adoption. Registered in `StrategyRegistry`, the T027 `actionable` gate refuses it (capability and operational evidence missing), matching the catalogue's `action_eligible: false`.

## Interpretations of unclear spec text
- **Drift bands.** "Contribution-first drift-band rebalance": cash goes to every positive underweight, and the band only decides which remaining deviations are reported as prerequisites (sales or funding). Band *triggers* are not modelled: a buy does not wait for a band breach. The band is absolute weight points of the base, and a deviation counts only when it is strictly greater than the band. The spec gives no band semantics or default, so the manifest default of 0 adds no hidden tolerance.
- **Post-flow capital base.** Taken as current cash plus holdings. Pending deposits are not in it, because unconfirmed transfers are prerequisites (§7).
- **Reserves (owner decision needed).** The reserve is part of the base but is not spendable. Consequence: with target weights summing to 1 and a non-zero reserve, the reserve can never be invested, so a `funding_required` prerequisite persists whenever the reserve exceeds band × base. The alternative (base excludes the reserve) is open for the owner.
- **Buy price.** The candidate's cost estimate uses the mark as supplied. The proposal gate (T026) re-prices and re-checks cash at publication.
- **Spending to target.** "Round down … recompute after each allocation": buys never exceed the target, so leftover cash below one lot stays as cash.

## Negative/adverse cases
- **Oracles.** NUM06: 5000 available, two 4000 targets. The first buy (by canonical id) gets 4000 and the second only 1000. NUM18: account 360 s old with a 300 s limit and a 1 s quote, so the run is blocked (`account_stale`).
- **Run-level blocks.** Missing, stale, future and other-currency marks of a held instrument; a conflicted account; a USD account in a CAD run; a future account observation; unadopted targets; no accounts; a short holding; a holding not confirmed unit-priced. Each blocks the run, with no base and no candidates.
- **Abstentions.** User-reported cash and a missing reserve each abstain without spending. Cash in acct-2 never buys an instrument permitted only in acct-1 (R043). Overweights are never bought, and the sale is a prerequisite only.
- **Refused inputs.** Weights summing to more than 1, a negative weight, a duplicate target, a lot that is not a power of ten, a negative cost, a duplicate tradability row, a band outside [0, 1], and a reserve in another currency.
- **Property (300 examples, input-derived oracle).**
  - Per account, Σ cash required ≤ max(cash − reserve, 0), and cash after = cash − spent.
  - Each quantity is a lot multiple at or above the minimum notional.
  - Each cost is the exact formula rounded up by less than 1e-12.
  - Only underweights are bought, and no buy passes its target.
  - Every instrument's distance to target does not grow.
  - Maximality: a targeted underweight with no candidate has an abstention reason, and no permitting account still had free cash for one lot (with the lot at or above the minimum notional and within the deficit).
  - The result is identical after shuffling every input mapping and tuple.
- **Mutation controls** (run on the working copy, restore verified by `diff`). At least one test failed for each: dropping the deficit bound; ignoring the reserve; reversing the tie-break; absolute ordering; accepting stale marks; spending user-reported cash; ignoring the minimum notional; ignoring `reconciled`; a non-strict or offset band comparison; sizing with a third of free cash; stopping after the first abstention. The one survivor, a cost-rounding retry loop, was dead code and became the fail-closed guard.
